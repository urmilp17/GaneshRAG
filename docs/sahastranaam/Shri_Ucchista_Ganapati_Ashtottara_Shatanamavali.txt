Shri Ucchista Ganapati Ashtottara Shatanamavali — 108 Names
Vandaruajanamandarapadapaya (वन्दारुजनमन्दारपादपाय) - Salutations to the One whose revered feet are like the heavenly Mandara tree.
Chandrardhashekharapranatanaya (चन्द्रार्धशेखरप्राणतनयाय) - Salutations to the dearest son of Lord Shiva, who bears the crescent moon; this name also indicates Ganapati as none other than Shiva, the manifestation of Brahman.
Shailarajasutotsangamandanaya (शैलराजसुतोत्सङ्गमण्डनाय) - Salutations to the son of Shakti, the daughter of the mountain king, created upon Her lap; this signifies the unity of Shiva and Shakti.
Vallishavalayakridakutukaya (वल्लीशवलयक्रीडाकुतुकाय) - Salutations to the One who playfully raced with His brother Kartikeya, showing that the entire cosmos is contained within the Divine.
Shrinilavanilalitarasikaya (श्रीनीलवाणीललितारसिकाय) - Salutations to the One embodying the essences of Lakshmi, Shakti and Sarasvati, together represented by Lalita Devi.
Svanandabhavananandanilayaya (स्वानन्दभवनानन्दनिलयाय) - Salutations to the One who dwells in the abode of supreme inner bliss, associated with the thousand-petalled lotus and the highest state of spiritual realization.
Chandramandalasandrisyasvarupaya (चन्द्रमण्डलसन्दृश्यस्वरूपाय) - Salutations to the One whose form is perceived within the disc of the moon.
Kshirabdhimadhyakalpadrumulasthaya (क्षीराब्धिमध्यकल्पद्रुमूलस्थाय) - Salutations to the One who stands beneath the wish-fulfilling Kalpataru in the centre of the ocean of milk.
Surapagasiambhojasamsthitaya (सुरापगासिताम्भोजसंस्थिताय) - Salutations to the One who stands upon a white lotus in the divine river.
Sadanikritamartandamandalaya (सदनीकृतमार्ताण्डमण्डलाय) - Salutations to the One who has made the solar disc His abode and is perceived as the divine Sun.
Ikshusagaramadhyasthamandiraya (इक्षुसागरमध्यस्थमन्दिराय) - Salutations to the One whose abode lies in the middle of an ocean of sugarcane juice.
Chintamanipuradhishasattamaya (चिन्तामणिपुराधीशसत्तमाय) - Salutations to the supreme Lord of Chintamani city, who fulfills the sincere wishes of devotees.
Jagatsrishtitirodanakaranaya (जगत्सृष्टितिरोधानकारणाय) - Salutations to the One who is the cause of creation and reabsorption of the universe.
Kridarthasrishtabhuvanatritayaya (क्रीडार्थसृष्टभुवनत्रितयाय) - Salutations to the One who creates the three worlds as a divine play.
Shundoddhutajalodbhutabhuvanaya (शुण्डोद्धूतजलोद्भूतभुवनाय) - Salutations to the One whose trunk sprinkles and nourishes the worlds born of His creation.
Chetanachetanibhutashariraya (चेतनाचेतनीभूतशरीराय) - Salutations to the One whose body is pure consciousness and who gives consciousness to individual beings.
Anumatrasharyantarlsitaya (अणुमात्रशरीरान्तर्लसिताय) - Salutations to the One who shines within even the smallest and subtlest forms of existence.
Sarvavashyakaranantamantrarnaya (सर्ववश्यकरानन्तमन्त्रार्णाय) - Salutations to the One whose mantra-syllables possess the power to attract and influence all things.
Kushthadyamayasantohashamanaya (कुष्ठाद्यामयसन्दोहशमनाय) - Salutations to the One who pacifies diseases and afflictions of sincere devotees.
Prativadimukhastambhakarakaya (प्रतिवादिमुखस्तम्भकारकाय) - Salutations to the One who silences the tongues of adversaries and protects devotees in contests and disputes.
Parabhicharadushkarmanashakaya (पराभिचारदुष्कर्मनाशकाय) - Salutations to the One who destroys harmful magical acts and evil directed against devotees.
Sakrinmantrajapadhyanamuktidaya (सकृन्मन्त्रजपध्यानमुक्तिदाय) - Salutations to the One who grants liberation through sincere mantra recitation and meditation.
Nijabhaktavipadrakshadikshitaya (निजभक्तविपद्रक्षादीक्षिताय) - Salutations to the One who personally protects His sincere devotees from danger.
Dhyanamritarasasvadadayakaya (ध्यानामृतरसास्वाददायकाय) - Salutations to the One who grants devotees the blissful essence experienced through meditation.
Guhyapujaratabhishtaphaladayaya (गुह्यपूजारताभीष्टफलदाय) - Salutations to the One who fulfills the wishes of devotees who sincerely perform His sacred worship.
Rupaudaryagunakrishtatrilokaya (रूपौदार्यगुणाकृष्टत्रिलोकाय) - Salutations to the One who attracts the three worlds through His beauty, nobility and divine qualities.
Ashtadravyahavihpritamanasaya (अष्टद्रव्यहविःप्रीतमानसाय) - Salutations to the One who is pleased by offerings of the eight sacred substances in fire worship.
Avatarashtakadvandvapradanaya (अवताराष्टकद्वन्द्वप्रदानाय) - Salutations to the One manifested among the principal forms of Lord Ganesha, including the Ucchista Ganapati form.
Bharatalekhanodbhinnaradanaya (भारतालेखनोद्भिन्नरदनाय) - Salutations to the One who broke His tusk to write the Mahabharata, symbolizing non-duality.
Naradodgitarucharitaritaya (नारदोद्गीतरुचिरचरिताय) - Salutations to the One whose glorious deeds are sung by the sage Narada.
Nikhilamnayasangushtavaibhavaya (निखिलाम्नायसङ्गुष्ठवैभवाय) - Salutations to the One whose greatness, particularly that associated with the thumb, is praised in the sacred traditions.
Banaravanachandishapujitaya (बाणरावणचण्डीशपूजिताय) - Salutations to the One worshipped by the mighty kings Bana and Ravana and by Chandisha, identified with Shiva.
Indradidevatavrindarakshakaya (इन्द्रादिदेवतावृन्दरक्षकाय) - Salutations to the One who protects Indra and the hosts of celestial beings.
Saptarshimanasalananishcheshtaya (सप्तर्षिमानसालाननिश्चेष्टाय) - Salutations to the One who remains firmly established in the minds of the seven great sages.
Adityadigrahastomadipakaya (आदित्यादिग्रहस्तोमदीपकाय) - Salutations to the One who illuminates the Sun, Moon and planets and is their source of light.
Madanagamasattantraparagaya (मदनागमसत्तन्त्रपारगाय) - Salutations to the One who has transcended desire and possesses mastery over the inner and outer realms.
Ujjiviteshasandagdhamadanaya (उज्जीवितेशसन्दग्धमदनाय) - Salutations to the One who revived Madana, the god of love, after he was burned by Shiva.
Shamimahiruhapritamanasaya (शमीमहीरुहप्रीतमानसाय) - Salutations to the One pleased by offerings of Shami leaves, symbolizing withdrawal from lower impulses toward higher spiritual pursuits.
Jalatarpanasampritahridayaya (जलतर्पणसम्प्रीतहृदयाय) - Salutations to the One whose heart is pleased by water-libation offerings.
Kandukikritakailashashikharaya (कन्दुकीकृतकैलासशिखराय) - Salutations to the One who playfully plays ball upon the summit of Mount Kailasa, representing the highest spiritual centre.
Atharvashirshakaranyamayuraya (अथर्वशीर्षकारण्यमयूराय) - Salutations to the One whose movement is compared to a peacock in the forest of the Atharvashirsha, identifying Him with its supreme teaching.
Kalyanachalashringagraviharaya (कल्याणाचलशृङ्गाग्रविहाराय) - Salutations to the One who wanders upon the auspicious peaks of Mount Meru, symbolizing the spiritual ascent toward the Sahasrara.
Atunaindradisamasamstutaya (आतुनैन्द्रादिसामसंस्तुताय) - Salutations to the One praised as Indra within the hymns of the Sama Veda.
Brahmyadimatrinivahaparitaya (ब्राह्म्यादिमातृनिवःपरीताय) - Salutations to the One surrounded by the eight Matrika forms of the Divine Mother.
Chaturthavaranarakshidigishaya (चतुर्थावरणारक्षिदिगीशाय) - Salutations to the One surrounded by the guardians of the cardinal directions in His fourth enclosure.
Dvaravishtanidhidvandvashobhitaya (द्वाराविष्टनिधिद्वन्द्वशोभिताय) - Salutations to the One whose doorway is adorned by the sacred treasures or Nidhis.
Anantaprithivikurmapithangaya (अनन्तपृथिवीकूर्मपीठाङ्गाय) - Salutations to the One whose pedestal encompasses space, earth and the cosmic tortoise, signifying His boundless nature.
Tivradıyoginivrindapithasthaya (तीव्रादियोगिनीवृन्दपीठस्थाय) - Salutations to the One whose pedestal is formed by the Yoginis and whose grace bestows spiritual powers.
Jayadinavapithashrimanditaya (जयादिनवपीठश्रीमण्डिताय) - Salutations to the One adorned by the sacred Pitha deities and associated with victory through their worship.
Panchavaranamadhyasthasadanaya (पञ्चावरणमध्यस्थसदनाय) - Salutations to the One who resides within the five sacred enclosures of His Yantra.
Kshetrapalaganeshadidvarapaya (क्षेत्रपालगणेशादिद्वारपाय) - Salutations to the One protected by Kshetrapala and other divine guardians at the entrance.
Mahiratiramagauriparshvakaya (महीरतीरमागौरीपार्श्वकाय) - Salutations to the One accompanied by Rati, Earth, Lakshmi and Gauri, signifying the fulfillment of material and spiritual aspirations.
Madyapriyadivinayividheyaya (मद्यप्रियादिविनयिविधेयाय) - Salutations to the One served by humble attendants such as Madyapriya.
Vanidurgamshabhutahrhakalatraya (वाणीदुर्गांशभूतार्हकलत्राय) - Salutations to the One possessing the powers of Vani and Durga, associated with knowledge, prosperity and courage.
Varahastipishachihriannandanaya (वरहस्तिपिशाचीहृन्नन्दनाय) - Salutations to the One who delights the heart of the divine feminine power known as Hastipishachi.
Yoginishachatushshashtisamyutaya (योगिनीशचतुष्षष्टिसंयुताय) - Salutations to the One accompanied by the sixty-four Yoginis, representing subtle spiritual energies.
Navadurgashtavasubhissevitaya (नवदुर्गाष्टवसुभिस्सेविताय) - Salutations to the One served by the nine forms of Durga and the eight Vasus.
Dvatramshadbhairavavyuhanayakaya (द्वात्रिंशद्भैरवव्यूहनायकाय) - Salutations to the One who leads the thirty-two Bhairavas and their hosts.
Airavatadidigdantisanvritaya (ऐरावतादिदिग्दन्तिसंवृताय) - Salutations to the One surrounded and served by Airavata and the other celestial elephants.
Kanthiravamayurakhuvahanaya (कण्ठीरवमयूराखुवाहनाय) - Salutations to the One who has the lion, peacock and mouse as vehicles, representing power, agility and universal reach.
Mushakangamaharaktaketanaya (मूषकाङ्कमहारक्तकेतनाय) - Salutations to the One whose red banner bears His beloved mouse vehicle.
Kumbhodarakaranyastapadabjaya (कुम्भोदरकरन्यस्तपादाब्जाय) - Salutations to the One whose lotus feet rest upon the hands of Kumbhodara, the lion-faced Gana.
Kantakantatarangasthakaragraya (कान्ताकान्ततराङ्गस्थकराग्राय) - Salutations to the One who places the tip of His trunk upon the radiant body of His beloved, symbolizing divine grace.
Antasthabhuvanasphitajatharaya (अन्तस्थभुवनस्फीतजठराय) - Salutations to the One whose vast stomach contains the worlds and symbolizes the cosmic universe.
Karpuravitikasararakhtoshthaya (कर्पूरवीटिकासाररक्तोष्ठाय) - Salutations to the One whose lips are reddened by the camphor-infused betel offered in worship.
Shvetarkamalasandiptakandharaya (श्वेतार्कमालासन्दीप्तकन्धराय) - Salutations to the One whose neck shines with a garland of white Shvetarka flowers.
Somasuryabrihadbhanulochanaya (सोमसूर्यबृहद्भानुलोचनाय) - Salutations to the One whose eyes represent the Moon, Sun and Fire.
Sarvasampatpradamandakatakshaya (सर्वसम्पत्प्रदामन्दकटाक्षाय) - Salutations to the One whose compassionate glance can bestow every form of prosperity.
Ativelamadaraktanayanaya (अतिवेलमदारक्तनयनाय) - Salutations to the One whose eyes are reddened by divine ecstasy and profound compassion.
Shashankardhasamadiptamastakaya (शशाङ्कार्धसमादीप्तमस्तकाय) - Salutations to the One whose head shines brilliantly beneath the crescent moon.
Sarpopavitaharadibhushitaya (सर्पोपवीतहारादिभूषिताय) - Salutations to the One adorned with a serpent as sacred thread and with precious ornaments.
Sinduritamahakumbhasuveshaya (सिन्दूरितमहाकुम्भसुवेषाय) - Salutations to the One beautifully adorned and anointed with sindura upon His great frontal lobes.
Ashavasanatadrishyaundaryaya (आशावसनतादृश्यसौन्दर्याय) - Salutations to the One whose beauty is visible in every direction and who is magnificently adorned.
Kantalimganasanajatapulakaya (कान्तालिङ्गनसञ्जातपुलकाय) - Salutations to the One who becomes ecstatic in the embrace of His beloved, representing the union of Shiva and Shakti.
Pashankushadhanurbanamanditaya (पाशाङ्कुशधनुर्बाणमण्डिताय) - Salutations to the One adorned with the noose, goad, bow and arrows.
Digantavyaptadanambusaurabhaya (दिगन्तव्याप्तदानाम्बुसौरभाय) - Salutations to the One whose fragrant divine exudation spreads throughout all directions.
Sayantanasahasramshuraktangaya (सायन्तनसहस्रांशुरक्ताङ्गाय) - Salutations to the One whose body is red and radiant like the thousand-rayed evening Sun.
Sampurnapranavakarasundaraya (सम्पूर्णप्रणवाकारसुन्दराय) - Salutations to the One whose beautiful form embodies the sacred Pranava, Om.
Brahmadikritayajnyagnisambhutaya (ब्रह्मादिकृतयज्ञाग्निसम्भूताय) - Salutations to the One manifested from the sacrificial fire of Brahma.
Sarvamaraprarthanattavigrahaya (सर्वामरप्रार्थनात्तविग्रहाय) - Salutations to the One who assumes His divine form through the prayers and worship of the gods.
Janimatrasuratrashanashakaya (जनिमात्रसुरत्रासनाशकाय) - Salutations to the One whose very birth dispelled the fears of the celestial beings.
Kalatrikritamatangakanyakaya (कलत्रीकृतमातङ्गकन्यकाय) - Salutations to the One associated with Matangi or Neela Sarasvati as His divine consort and Shakti.
Vidyavadasuraprananashakaya (विद्यावदसुरप्राणनाशकाय) - Salutations to the One who destroyed the demon Vidyavat and protects devotees from harmful forces.
Sarvamantrasamaradhyasvarupaya (सर्वमन्त्रसमाराध्यस्वरूपाय) - Salutations to the One whose very form is worshipped through all mantras and who is invoked before other divine worship.
Shatkonayantrapithantarlasitaya (षट्कोणयन्त्रपीठान्तर्लसिताय) - Salutations to the One who shines within the six-cornered Yantra formed by two intersecting triangles.
Chaturnavatimantratmavigrahaya (चतुर्नवतिमन्त्रात्मविग्रहाय) - Salutations to the One whose form is constituted through ninety-four sacred mantras.
Humgamklamglammukhanekabijarnaya (हुं गं क्लां ग्लां मुखानेकबीजार्णाय) - Salutations to the One represented by numerous sacred seed syllables such as Hum, Gam, Klam and Glam.
Bijaksharatrayantastashariraya (बीजाक्षरत्रयान्तस्थशरीराय) - Salutations to the One whose form is established within the three seed sounds A, U and M forming Om.
Hril lekhaguhyamantrantarbhavitaya (हृल्लेखागुह्यमन्त्रान्तर्भाविताय) - Salutations to the One embodied in the secret mantra Hrim, representing the dynamic power of Shakti.
Svahantamatrikamalarupadhyaya (स्वाहान्तमातृकामालारूपाध्याय) - Salutations to the One adorned with the garland of Sanskrit letters, representing the manifested sound of Brahman.
Dvatramshadaksharamayapratikaya (द्वात्रिंशदक्षरमयप्रतीकाय) - Salutations to the One whose magnificent form is represented by His thirty-two-syllabled mantra.
Shodhananarthasanmantravisheshaya (शोधनानर्थसन्मन्त्रविशेषाय) - Salutations to the One whose proper mantras are presented as requiring no additional purification or suitability rituals.
Ashtangayoginirvanadayakaya (अष्टाङ्गयोगिनिर्वाणदायकाय) - Salutations to the One who grants liberation through the practice of Ashtanga Yoga.
Pranendriyamanobuddhiprerakaya (प्राणेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिप्रेरकाय) - Salutations to the One who activates the breath, senses, mind, intellect and organs of life.
Muladharavarakshtranayakaya (मूलाधारवरक्षेत्रनायकाय) - Salutations to the One who governs the Muladhara, the foundational chakra.
Chaturdalamahapadmasamvishtaya (चतुर्दलमहापद्मसंविष्टाय) - Salutations to the One seated upon the mystical four-petalled lotus of the Muladhara.
Mulatrikonasambhobhipavakaya (मूलत्रिकोणसंशोभिपावकाय) - Salutations to the One who shines like fire within the sacred triangle of the Muladhara.
Sushumnarandhrasanchardeshikaya (सुषुम्नारन्ध्रसञ्चारदेशिकाय) - Salutations to the One who facilitates the opening of the Sushumna channel for the ascent of Kundalini.
ShatgranthinimnatatinItarakaya (षट्ग्रन्थिनिम्नतटिनीतारकाय) - Salutations to the One who facilitates the opening of the six chakras by overcoming their subtle knots.
Daharakashasambhoshashashankaya (दहराकाशसंशोभिशशाङ्काय) - Salutations to the One who illuminates the inner heart-space like the radiance of the Moon.
Hiranmayapurambhojanilayaya (हिरण्मयपुराम्भोजनिलयाय) - Salutations to the One who dwells within the golden lotus of the heart.
Bhrumadhyakomal aramakokilaya (भ्रूमध्यकोमलारामकोकिलाय) - Salutations to the One who dwells between the eyebrows like a gentle cuckoo, representing the Ajna chakra.
Shannavadvadashantasthamartandaya (षण्णवद्वादशान्तस्थमार्ताण्डाय) - Salutations to the One present at the subtle points six, nine and twelve inches above the head.
Manonmanisukhavasanirvritaya (मनोन्मणीसुखावासनिर्वृताय) - Salutations to the One who comfortably resides in the Manonmani chakra and transcends the ordinary mind.
Shodashantamahapadmamadhupaya (षोडशान्तमहापद्ममधुपाय) - Salutations to the One who resembles a bee dwelling upon the great lotus situated above the head.
Sahasrarasudhasarasechitaya (सहस्रारसुधासारसेचिताय) - Salutations to the One bathed in the nectar flowing from the thousand-petalled Sahasrara lotus.
Nadabindudvayatitasvarupaya (नादबिन्दुद्वयातीतस्वरूपाय) - Salutations to the One whose ultimate nature transcends Nada and Bindu and the duality represented by them.
Ucchishtagananathayamaheshaya (उच्छिष्टगणनाथायमहेशाय) - Salutations to the Great Lord, the Master of the Ganas and Mahesha Himself; Ucchista Ganapati represents the supreme Whole, the ultimate reality beyond creation and manifestation.
