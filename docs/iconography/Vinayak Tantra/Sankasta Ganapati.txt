71. Sankasta Ganapati

He has the lustre which is equal to the well heated refined gold, one of his tusks are broken, the lord has elephant face, he has four hands he shows the gesture of wish giving, and gesture of protection, he holds lasso and elephant goad in his hands. His eyes are bright like the shining and burning fire, he is flanked by his ghanas with chowries. I comtemplate on samkatanaSana - the destroyer of sorrows and dangers who holds the serpant and has mouse as his flag symbol.

Pleased by the austerity of the deity of krsnapaksacaturthi, lord ganesha blessed her with different kind of boons. He is sankastavinayaka.

The lord is the destroyer of all kinds of dangers and pains.
