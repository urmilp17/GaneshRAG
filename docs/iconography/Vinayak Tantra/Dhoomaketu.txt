117. Dhoomaketu

He who is red in color, holds axe, discus, lotus and conch in his four hands and is emitting fire from his mouth, he who has a flag which is smoky in color. He is in the form of a young little boy incarnated as the son of madhava for killing dhuma (the smoke demon) the lord is elephant faced and has two tusks. I worship that dhumaketu (he who smoke as his flag).

Ganesha who incarnated as the son of madhava and sumada is known as dhumaketu. He is the one who destroyed the demon dhuma. (Described in the dhyana shloka).

Dhoomaketu is the destroyer of all enemies (internal and external).
