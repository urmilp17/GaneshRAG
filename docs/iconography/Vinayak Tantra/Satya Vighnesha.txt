87. Satya Vighnesha

I worship Satya Ganapati who is in the complexion of vermilion, has his tusk, lasso, elephant goad and a garland. He ties a snake in his belly as his belt. He wears a pearl garland, he has one broken tusk (which he holds in one of his hands) he is together with his consorts satyasarasvati and satyalakshmi.

Lord Ganesha - the consort of siddhi (the goddess of success) and buddhi (the goddess of intellect), who manifested as the son of brahma the creator is known as satyavighnesvara in tantra shastras. He is the lord who makes the words of his devotees come true and makes his devotees adhere by truth.
