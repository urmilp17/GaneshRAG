42. Kalipriya Ganapati

Do worship the lord kalipriya whose body is white in complexion, who is mounted on a horse who is shining with a great two layered armor, his hair is thick and round (curly) and has
great lustre, his head is wrapper with a turban (headgear) made of cloth. He has bright glowing eyes, and has two hands, has a human face and is handsome like manmatha (the god of lust and love)

For the welfare mortals living in the era of kali in non indian lands, who does not follow the arya dharma (the path of vedas, pruanas and tantras), many gods appeared. All those non-Hindu gods are also worshipped as ganesha by the ganapatyas refered as kalipriya Ganapati.
