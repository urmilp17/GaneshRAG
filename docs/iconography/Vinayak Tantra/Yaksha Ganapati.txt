105. Yaksha Ganapati

He has a body which has golden complexion and lustre, he is elephant faced
and wears the moon on his crest, holds, lasso, elephant goad and shows the gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection with his hands. He looks at the lotus face of yakshini. I remember in my heart the lord yaksa ganeshwara who has three eyes.

Brahma Vishnu and mahendra were stuck by pride in a previous yuga. Then lord ganesha who is the supreme light assumed the form of a yaksha and appeared before them and took back his power residing in them. Agni and other gods failed before him, goddess parvati appeared and told the gods that he is Brahman - the supreme reality.

After hearing it, they all sought refuge in him. Ganesha appeared before them
and gave back their powers and disappeared again along with yakshini (his
consort).

The lord is the giver of the wisdom of the three worlds. (i.e) the accurate knowledge about the past, present and future happenings pertaining to all three worlds.
