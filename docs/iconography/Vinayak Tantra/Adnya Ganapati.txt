10. Adnya Ganapati

I worship him who is white in complexion and has four hands, he has the moon as his ornament (on his crest), he is seated in the posture of virasana, and his left hand is touching his left knee. In his right hand he is showing the great mudra (gesture) — chinmudra — the gesture of wisdom. He 1s holding elephant goad and a branch of mango tree with mangoes, he is with his eternal consorts Mahavidya (embodiment of great knowledge) and maharadnyi (embodiment of queenhood).

The lord is prayed for subduing the worlds, making the worlds favorable and for obtaining all kinds of royal fortune, and wealth.
