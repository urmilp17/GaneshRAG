9. Navneeta Ganapati

I remember the Lord Ganapati having pure white complexion and he is wearing the moon as his crestal ornament, he has three eyes. He is adorned with a white garment, he is accompanied by his consorts Shri (the goddess of wealth and success) and Vani (the goddess of speech, words and knowledge) he is the one who gave boons to rame§a (the lord of Rama — Vishnu) he is the embodiment of amruta (the tonix which stops death) he holds amruta (in a golden vessel) , lotus, elephant goad and mallet (mudgara - a hammer like weapon) he is elephant faced and 1s worshipped by gods and sages. 

The lord Navaneeta Ganapati is the great one he has four hands, he is the one who blesses all the gods. He mani fested with this form by his own will. The lord is also known as Sudhaa Ganapati or Amruta Ganapati (the embodiment of the nectar of the gods) 

The lord is worshipped for protection from aging and death (sudden death), and for good health.
