115. Ajnya Ganapati

He has his hand touching his knee, he has great sugarcane, and he shows a gesture of wisdom. He is seated on the throne, he is adorned by samrajni and maharajni on his sides, they are golden in complexion, the lord is moony in complexion, bears a moon on his crest and he is elephant faced. 1 worship that ajnyaganesa

For a days time in brahma's life (time period marked as a day of lord brahma) lord Ganapati descends from the chintamanidvipa (the island made of wish fulfilling gems) to svarga svananda bhavana (his heavenly palace of the bliss of the self) along with his attendants (the Ghanas) and devas (gods and goddesses) he leads a court there.

He is the giver of royal wealth, leadership and controllership.
