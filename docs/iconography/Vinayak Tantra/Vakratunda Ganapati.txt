3. Vakratunda Ganapati

I contemplate in my heart the one who has the glow of the rising sun, he holds lasso, elephant goad, his broken tusk and shows the gesture of protection in his four hands, he has three eyes and is wearing the crescent moon, he is adorned with the wish fulfilling gem (chintamani) on his neck, he has the complexion of the early morning sun (aruna).

He is the lord residing in chintamanidvipa (an island made of wish fulfilling gems) in the mulasthana

The supreme lord who has siddhi and buddhi powers under his control he is indeed vakratunda, the lord residing in mulasthana (mooladhara chakra).
