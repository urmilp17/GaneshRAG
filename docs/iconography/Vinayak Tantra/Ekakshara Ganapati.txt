1. Ekakshara Ganapati

The lord is the eternal truth, the embodiment of ultimate bliss, his abode is also made of truth and bliss. He lives there seated on a red lotus in the centre of the bright light which is symbol of truth. He is the lord of the impediments, he is pure, he is reddish in color. He is the eternal witness. His form from below the neck to feet represents saguna Brahman (god with qualities and characteristics), his head represents absolute brahmam (not bound to form or qualities) He is both saguna and nirguna. He is embraced by his beloved eternal consorts, he shows gesture of wish giving and complete protection, in the upper two hands and holds lasso and elephant goad in his lower two hands. I worship that ekaksara Ganesha. 

Ganesha Tantra mentions that 
ekaksarasya mantrasya svanandesastu devata

The deity of the one syllable mantra is svanandesha - the lord of the abode of 
svananda loka. 

visuddhasatvamayaya janako gananayakah 
ekaksaramurtiriti kathyate tantraparagaih
 
The creator (father) of vishuddhasatva maya (maya of purest satva) is said as 
ekaksaramurti by the ones who are well versed and knowledgable in tantra.
