95. Ghanta Ganapati

He has an elephant face, he wears ornaments made of nine precious gems and dressed with pure white cloth. He holds a lotus in his trunk, book, lasso, bell, and elephant goad in four of his hands, he bears a piece of moon on his crest, he has three eyes and has the complexion and radiance of pure moon white color, I contemplate him who is known as chitraghanta who is accompanied with goddess nadasarasvati.

Ghanta Ganapati is the giver of popularity , fame, knowledge and glory.
