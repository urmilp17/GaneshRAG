25. Panchadeva Varada

I seek lord maytresvara Ganapati for attaining all kinds of wealth and all desires, the lord who gave boons to the five great gods (brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, surya and Shakti)

He is red in complexion, he shows the gesture of wish giving, holds lasso, his broken tusk, and elephant goad in his hands, he has his consorts (siddhi and buddhi) on both his sides, he is adorned by a red garment, his mount is peacock.

He who blessed the five gods with boons is svanandanayaka himself.

He is praied for attaining all wealth and accomplishments.
