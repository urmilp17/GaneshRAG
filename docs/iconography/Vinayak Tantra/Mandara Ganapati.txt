120. Mandara Ganapati

I bow to the lord mandara ganapati who is mounted on a big mouse, has an elephant face. He has golden complexion and is wearing an yellow garment, he has two tusks and long ears, he has three eyes, he holds lasso, elephant goad and shows gestures of wish giving and protection with his four hands. he is always accompanied by his consorts sarvabhistapradatri (she who fulfils all the wishes of her devotees) and arvasamkastahantri (she who destroys all the sorrows and pains).

The lord who emerged from roots of the mandara tree (one of the five types of wish giving trees of the heaven) that bears beautiful flowers of golden color and killed tapasura is mandara gananayaka.

Mandara Gananayaka is the destroyer of physical and mental diseases and sorrows.
