66. Kala Ganapati

He is showing the gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection , he holds tusk an a book, and with other two hands he who holds club and trident, he is black like the dark rainy clouds, he has a great elephant face, I worship that lord named kala who bears the crescent moon on his crest.

The god who is in the form of millisecond to brahma kalpa (period life time of one brahma) is called kala (the god of time)

The lord ganesha - the soul of kala who emerged from the heart centre of kala pleased by his penance and austerity is kala Ganapati.

He is the lord who leads the wheel of time.
