48. Panchamukha Ganapati

I contemplate on panchamukha Ganesha who bears moon on his crest. He is as white as the sphatika stone, wears a garland of headless bodies, has three eyes, and holds snake, lasso, bell, fire in the time of pralaya, elephant goad, trident, vajra, axe, gesture of protection, and gesture of wish giving. wears snakes as various ornaments, one who blessed lord Shiva to win over the three demon cities.

Without worshipping lord Ganesha, lord Shiva started war against Tripurasura. He was heavily afflicted and troubled by impediments and hindrances, he couldn't defeat tripurasura in spite of tremendous efforts. He was facing defeat. Then lord Shiva did great penance of twelve years, meditating upon lord Ganesha as advised by sage narada. The light of supreme godhood (param Brahman) in the heart centre grew by the merit and power of his austerity. The light came of through all his five faces and manifested as five elephant headed Ganesha.

The lord gunesha, residing in the heart of shiva in the form of light of the soul manifested as heramba. He initiated the thousand names hymn (sahasranama stotram) of Ganesha to lord Shiva. By the power gained through chanting that great hymn, by the grace of lord Heramba ganesha, lord sadashiva destroyed the Tripura. This five headed ganesha none other than Shiva’s atman (soul) in the form of elephant lord (Ganesha)

The lord Panchamukha Ganapati is the destroyer of the bondage of the three bodies (physical, subtle and causal) he is the giver of ultimate wisdom and knowledge and final liberation.
