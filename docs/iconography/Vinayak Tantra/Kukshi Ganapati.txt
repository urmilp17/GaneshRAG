59. Kukshi Ganapati

I worship the kuksi ganesha having an elephant face, who is bright red in lustre and color like the hibiscus flower, hold lasso, elephant goad, gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection.

He has his tusk in the hand which shows the gesture of protection, he has a big belly with a large navel hole (belly button)

To destroy the pride and ego of brahma, Vishnu and mahesha, lord ganesha took a form of a small boy and stood in the ocean. The three gods went near him drenched in the water of the ocean. The entered the little boy’s stomach through (sucked by ) the hole in the navel. They witnessed billions and trillions of universes inside the belly. They were drowned in the ocean inside the belly (where the universes were floating), they left their pride and ego and praised Ganesha with hymns. By the grace of the lord, they got out of lord’s stomach.

This form of ganesha, is called kuksiganesa, he is the one who rectifies all the stomach, digestion related diseases.
