43. Ganaka guru

He has a crystal white body, he holds a book. Rosary, lotus and shows the gesture of wish giving and gesture of wisdom (chinmudra) in his hands. He is like the bright sun to the darkness of birth (mundane life cycle. He has a beautiful moon on his crest, he is the knower of all high spiritual truths, I bow to bim whois in the form of Hamsa (the state of I am he - non dual state with supreme reality).

Sage ganaka is the form of gunesha, who incarnated in different forms on earth. Ganaka is the the first and foremost guru who is the teacher of scriptures of the ganapatya tradition.

Ganaka guru is the giver of sacred knowledge and wisdom.
