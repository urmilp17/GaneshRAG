37. Mahotkat Vinayak

The lord mahotkat holds a staff, trident, carrying a sacred water-jar, lasso, elephant goad, lotus and shows gesture of complete protection and wish giving. He rides a lion. He is in golden complexion, bears a moon on crest, has three eyes and wears silk. He wears a garland of pearls which touches his chest. I contemplate on him in my heart centre.

The lord who incarnated as the son of sage kashyapa, to destroy devantaka
and other demons is Mahotkat.

Mahotkat is the lord who makes his devotees accomplish all kinds of accomplishments.
