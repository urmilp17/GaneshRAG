6. Adnyachakra Ganapati

Lord mahaganapati has elephant face, curvy trunk, three eyes and wears the moon as his crestal ornament, he holds pomegranate fruit, mace, sugar cane bow, trident, disc, lotus, lasso, blue lotus, a kind of rice grain, tusk, a jewel jar, accompanied by siddhilaksmi to his left and mahavidya and mahamaya to his right. He 1s served by the four pairs in the four directions. The four pairs are rama and ramesha, uma and ma heshwara, mahi and varaha, rati and kandarpa (manmatha) I contemplate him on my adnya chakra.

When he comes in control of goddess siddhi, that time, he is the one resides adnyasthana (adnya chakra).
