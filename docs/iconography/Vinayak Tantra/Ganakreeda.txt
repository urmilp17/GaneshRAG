20. Ganakreeda

He who is residing in a bright red lotus which is on a boat which is in the middle of a red ocean. He has three eyes. He holds lasso, elephant goad, axe and gesture of protection in his hands. He is immersed in his own bliss (the bliss of self realization bliss of experiencing god in oneself) He has elephant like face and a big belly. He is accompanied by goddesses Siddhi and Buddhi. He is red in complexion; he bears sun and moon on his crest. He has the
nature of rectifying all fears. He is the king of all obstacles. I bow to him.

The bliss of self is ganakrida
Ganakreeda is the form of ganesha who helps us and guides us in the form of guru (spiritual master) to attain all four accomplishments and bless us with the bliss of self.
