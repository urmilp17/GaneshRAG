122. Gajadanta Ganapati

He - gajamukha - the elephant faced lord is holding his tusk, a beautiful lotus, lasso and elephant goad in his hands and having a big coconut in his trunk, he is shining with pearl ornaments, he has the complexion equal like that of ivory. He is called the gajadanta Ganapati, he has a crescent moon on his crest, I always contemplate on him and visualise him with the goddesses gajavidya (embodiment and deity of knowledge related to elephants) and gajalaksmi. (embodiment and deity of elephant wealth).

The lord who emerged from ivory is called gajadantaganesvara.
