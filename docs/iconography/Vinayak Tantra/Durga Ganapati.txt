54. Durga Ganapati

He holds trident, lasso, discus and elephant goad in his four hands, he is adorned with moon, and he has an elephant head, he is red in complexion, he is adorned with fragrant pastes and clothes which are reddish like the Hibiscus flowers. I worship that durga ganapati.

Long ago, during the battle with mahisha the buffalo demon, goddess durga was afflicted by him. Goddess durga reached mount vindhya and there she did great penance and remained in austerity and meditated on ganesha, lord ganesha emerged from the heart lotus of goddess durga and lord ganesha the lord of siddhis (success and accomplishments) blessed her with the strength and power to slay mahisha the buffalo demon. This form of ganesha is known as durga Ganapati he who destroys all the dangers and difficulties.

Durga Ganapati is the lord who destroys all sins, difficulties and dangers.
