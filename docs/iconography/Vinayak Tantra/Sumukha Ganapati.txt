80. Sumukha Ganapati

The lord holds the pomegranate fruit, tusk, staff, lotus, bow, blue lotus, trident, lasso, discus, and conch and holds a Jewel jar in his trunk, he has the complexion of noon plant's flower, and he is together with Siddhi and Buddhi. I contemplate on Sumukha Ganapati who always blesses Bhuvaneshi with all that she desires.

Lord Ganesha who appeared from the meditation of goddess bhuvanesi to bless her is known as sumukha. He is accompanied by two shaktis (powers embodied as goddesses)

Sumukha is the lord who makes our lives auspicious and promotes happiness.
