88. Rakta Ganapati

Lord Rakta Ganapati has a reddish complexion, he holds a trident, discus, axe, lotus, vreehi (a kind of rice grain ripening in the rainy season), lasso, and bow made of sugarcane, mace, elephant goad and blue lotus in his hands and holds a jewel jar in his trunk. He rides a lion, he looks towards the faces of sundari (the goddess of beauty and elegance) and bharati (the goddess of wisdom and brightness) the lord wears ornaments of pearls. He has a mark in his forehead made of mrugamada (musk), wears moon in his crest and has three eyes. I contemplate on him.

The colors of maya are described in the vedas.

She is one, birthless and is red, white and black in color. These are the colors of the three modes (satva ~ white, rajas - red and tamas - black)

Three colors of maya - the supreme power of supreme god manifested (appeared) in fire (red), water (white) and earth (black), the first of the three is red color. The deity of the red color worshiped lord Ganesha. Pleased by the austerity of the red color deity lord Ganesha happily appeared before him. The form in which lord ganesha appeared before deity of red color is Rakta Ganapati (Red Ganesha).

He is the lord who makes all the worlds favorable for the devotees.
