100. Jyeshtaraja Ganapati

He has eight hands, he is surrounded by hari (Vishnu) Shiva, bhuvana (shakti) surya (sun) his court is filled by ka (Brahma - creator), A (Vishnu) and eesha (Rudra - destroyer) of crores and crores of universes. The lord watches his court with grace, 1 contemplate on him in my heart centre, who is pure white in complexion and bears a moon on his crest. The lord is elephant faced, and is seated on a throne. He is known jayéstharaja. He is accompanied by virajni and svarajni.

The lord Ganesha who incarnated as the son of the king of kurus is known as jayestaraja

He is the one who blesses the devotees with kingly wealth and fortunes. (or kingship or queenhood)
