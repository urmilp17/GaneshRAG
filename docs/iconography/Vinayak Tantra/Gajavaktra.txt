14. Gajavaktra

I contemplate on the elephant faced lord who has pearl white skin tone, has a great elephant face, three eyes and wears a moon on his crest, one who is the lord of liberation and shows the gesture of wish giving and complete protection and holds an elephant goad and a great lasso. The birth-less lord is always accompanied by his consorts jnanabuddhi (the embodiment of mind with ultimate wisdom) and mokshasiddhi (attainment of liberation) and resides in all the principles of creation described in samkhya philosophy.

This is the form of lord ganesha residing in all the tattvas (principles)

He is the giver of ultimate wisdom and liberation.
