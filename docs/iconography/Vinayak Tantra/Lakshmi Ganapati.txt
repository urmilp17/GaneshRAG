98. Lakshmi Ganapati

The lord holds lasso, kalpa tree (wish giving tree), a jar of rubies, elephant goad, gesture of wish giving and complete protection, lotus and pomegranate fruit. He is in the color of pure heated gold and is together with saubhagyada and putrada, bears moon in his crest, and has three eyes. I worship laksmiganésa.

Mahalakshmi - the wife of Vishnu, to get manmatha the god of love as her son worshipped ganesha through penance and austerity and meditated upon him on her heart centre. Ganesha appeared and gave her the boons she desired with pleasure. By the grace of ganesha, she attained manmatha as her son.

When manmatha was burnt by the fire of rudra’s eyes in the previous yuga, by the instructions and order of goddess mahalaksmi, manmata's wife goddess rati worshiped Lakshmi ganesha and pleased him with penance and austerity and attained her fortune back by the grace of brahmanaspati.

This form of ganesha is the giver of good fortune and offspring.
