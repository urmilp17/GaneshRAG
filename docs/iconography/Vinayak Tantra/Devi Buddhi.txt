I meditate upon the Supreme Buddhi, radiant in white, holding a lotus bud, with a smiling face, adorned in pure white garments. Her right hand is gracefully extended, she is crowned with the crescent moon, and her lofty bosom is adorned with a fitted bodice. She is united with the loving glance of Vighneśa, stands at the right side of Gaṇapati, wears a garland of campaka flowers, is praised by Brahmā and Īśa, and shines as the Supreme Buddhi whom I contemplate.
