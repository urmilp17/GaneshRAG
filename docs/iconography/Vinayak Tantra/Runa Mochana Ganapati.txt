124. Runa Mochana Ganapati

I bow to the god, the lord of all ghanas( primary holy attendants of lord shiva) , who has vermillion complexion and has two hands, having a big belly and is seated on lotus petals. The lord who is worshipped and served even by the gods brahma the creator and others, and is accompanied by siddhas.

As per the krushna yamala tantra. He is the form of ganesha who frees the devotees from debts and loans.
