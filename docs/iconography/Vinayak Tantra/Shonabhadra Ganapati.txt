91. Shonabhadra Ganapati

The lord is elephant faced and he has shanka nidhi and Padma nidhi by his sides. He holds lasso, elephant goad, axe and lotus in his four hands, he is bright and has the reddish hue of sonabhadra. He is holding a big pot embellished and filled with precious gems with his trunk, the lord. Worship the lord shonabhadra ganesha

The lord who emerged from the sacred shonabhadra stones is shonabhadra ganesha also known as dehalibhava ganesha.

He who gives everything for his devotees.
