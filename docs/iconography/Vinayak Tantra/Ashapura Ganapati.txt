85. Ashapura Ganapati

I do remembrance of asapura Ganesha who has elephant face, he has the radiance of Indian laurel flower, has a reddish head (the color of sindhoora). He rides a peacock. Has a snake as his belly belt. He is together with aasapurasarasvati and asaptralaksmi. He wears a garland of pearls and has ten arms and wears a bright cloth.

There is a great sage seer and a siddha named bhrushundi, he attained the form of ganesha through his true devotion for ganesha. The form in which lord emerged from bhrushundi's meditation and visualization is Ashapura Ganapati. He is the lord who fulfils all our wishes and desires.
