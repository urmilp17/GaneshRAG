51. Sahastramukha Ganapati

I worship the lord sahasrasya ganésa who has appearance of reddish complexion like the color of japapushpa (China rose), has thousand heads, two thousand hands and three thousand eyes (three eyes per face) one who has fragrant pastes applied in him and is adorned by a red cloth.

The lord hiranyagarbha created the world made of five great elements and he entered it. He is known as virat purusha.

Elephant headed Lord gunesha who manifested with thousand faces by the meditation and Visualization done by virat purusha is called sahasrasya. He is the supreme lord who is the soul of virat purusha.

He is indeed the lord worshiped always by virabhadra in his heart. Sahasramukha Ganapati is the one who wards off all dangers and troubles.
