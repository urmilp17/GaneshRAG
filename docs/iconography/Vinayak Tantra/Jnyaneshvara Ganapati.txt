114. Jnyaneshvara Ganapati

He has elephant face, wears the moon on his crest, he shines like the pure crystal gem, shows the gesture of wisdom (chinmudra), holds a rosary, lasso and elephant goad in his four hands and is sitting in the the heroic posture (veerasana) , accompanied by bramhavidya (the wisdom of supreme absolute god) and dhyanasiddhi (the attainment of meditatative state). I contemplate on that Jnyana Ganapati who has three eyes and both tusks on his face.

The lord Ganesha who manifested from the meditation of prakruti (primordial nature) and purusha (personification of universal consciousness) is jnyaneswara Ganapati. He is the embodiment of ultimate wisdom. He is the giver of ultimate wisdom to lord shiva and lord dattatreya yogi. He is the giver or wisdom. He is the one who gives the real wisdom of supreme reality (Brahman).
