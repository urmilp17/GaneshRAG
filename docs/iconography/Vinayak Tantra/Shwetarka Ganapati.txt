70. Shwetarka Ganapati

He holds in his lotus like hands, pomegranate, lasso, elephant goad and his tusk, he has a shiny white garment wirh golden hue, he is born from the root of Svetarka ( Calotropis Gigantea Alba). He lord has the mouse as his mount, he has elephant face, he is accompanied with two shaktis (his powers personified as goddesses) he has a white hue, he has moon as his crestal ornament, and has three eyes, I worship that lord Shvetarka ganesha.

The lord ganesha who appeared from the roots of svetarka tree (Calotropis Gigantea Alba) for killing the demon gandhacanda pleased by the penance and austerity of the gods is called swetarka ganapati.
