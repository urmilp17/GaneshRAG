86. Kalpaka Ganapati

He holds trident, disc, bow, mace, wish granting tree, tusk, a kind of rice grain, blue lotus, lasso, lotus in his ten hands, he bears moon in crest, he is red in complexion, has three eyes, I contemplate now on kalpaka ganapati who is accompanied by kalpakamahavani and kalpakarama.

kalpaka ganesha is the son of goddess sundari.
He is the giver of all that we wish for.
