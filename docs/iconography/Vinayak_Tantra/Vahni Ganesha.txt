77. Vahni Ganesha

I worship the vahni Ganapati who 15 accompanied by rupalaksmi (the goddess of beauty and good appearance), he who holds his tusk, shakti weapon, a vessel of ghee clarified butter, a beautiful pot embellished with gems in his four hands and a big modaka sweet in his trunk . One who has elephant face and is shining with golden complexion.

The goddess in whom Charm, good appearance and beauty rests is called rupalaksmi in vedas and other sacred scriptures. The lord ganesha who appeared from the centre of the fire along with rupalaksmi as his consort is called vahniganesa by those who have realized the wisdom of vedas.

This form of ganesha is worshiped for attaining good looks.
