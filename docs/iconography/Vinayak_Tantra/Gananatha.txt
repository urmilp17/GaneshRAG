21. Gananatha

He is bright in the color of sindhoora (vermilion), has three eyes, big belly and is covered by (wears) red cloth. He holds lasso, elephant goad, his broken tusk and shows the gesture of giving protection from all fears. He is embraced by Siddhi and Buddhi (the goddesses of success and wisdom) he has a great elephant like face, he has one tusk (broken), he is adorned by all the ornaments, I contemplate on Ganesha who blesses his people with bliss and happiness.

gananatha is the brahman who is not bound by forms and attributes.

This is the form of lord ganesha who is the foundation of existence and the giver of ultimate wisdom.
