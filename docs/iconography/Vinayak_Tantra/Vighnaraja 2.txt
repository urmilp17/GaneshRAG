121. Vighnaraja

i worship vighnaraja (the king of obstacles (i.e) the leader and controller of
obstacles and impediments) who holds conch , discus, elephant goad, flower
arrows, lasso, sugarcane bow, axe, rice grain bunch, with his lotus hands, and
always mounted on a mouse, who is appearing in golden complexion, wears moon on his crest, has three eyes and is accompanied by his consorts siddhalaksmi (goddess who is the embodiment of the wealth of the accomplished ones) and vagisvari (the mistress of words and speech) who has a majestic elephant face.

Lord Ganesha incarnated as the son of parsva and killed vighnasura (the obstacle demon). The yogis call this form of Ganesha as vighnaraja (the king and controller of obstacles and hindrances) in the sacred scriptures.

He is the destroyer of even the greatest obstacles.
