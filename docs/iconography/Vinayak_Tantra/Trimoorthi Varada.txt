27. Trimoorthi Varada

He is reddish is complexion like the Pentapetes Phoenicea flower. He bears the moon on his crest, he wears a red garment, has an elephant face, holds sword, bow, shield, Shakti weapon, in his hands, he who blesses ka (brahma) a (Vishnu) and eesha (rudra), he is accompanied by prakruti (the goddess and embodiment of primordial nature) and vidya (goddess and embodiment of wisdom). I always meditate upon that lord who is known as vakratunda.

The lord vakratunda who blessed the divine trinity with the powers of creation, protection and destruction. The lord is the giver of wealth and wisdom in this form.
