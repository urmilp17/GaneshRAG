62. Margesha Ganapati

He holds the discus (sudarshana), tusk, trident and pomegranate and a big modaka sweet with his trunk. He is riding an elephant he is the first and foremost, he is dark in complexion, I worship that margesha ganesa - the lord of the path, the lord of the way.

In the path of liberation resides the gods and goddesses, brahma, Vishnu, shiva, Shakti and surya, Ganesha is the greatest of all as paths for liberation. He is called margesa Ganapati.

Margesa Ganapati protects us and guides us through our paths. The lord also protects us during travel.
