76. Mukta Ganapati

One who wears a long garland of pearls and is adorned with ornaments with nine precious gems, he holds a sacred pot of amrita in his trunk. He is blue in complexion and has three eyes. He holds a weapon called shakti which has many pearls embedded in it, a lotus, lasso and his tusk in four of his hands, I bow to muktaganesha who bears a crown made of precious stones and is accompanied by muktalaksmi.

Once lord Vishnu meditated on goddess Mahalakshmi who protects and
nurtures all the worlds. she is the goddess residing in the heart of Vishnu. By the effect of Vishnu’s meditation, Lakshmi manifested from the centre of the pearls from the milky ocean and was born as the daughter of lord Vishnu. She was also named Lakshmi. Since she manifested from the pearls she is called muktalaksmi. Lord Ganesha accepted muktalaksmi in marriage from lord Vishnu. He is called PashaPaniGanesha (ganesha who has a noose in his hand). This is the form of ganesha worshipped by sagara (the deity of ocean.)

He is the giver of glories and wealth.
