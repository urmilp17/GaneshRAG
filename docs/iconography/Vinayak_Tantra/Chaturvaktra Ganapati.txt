47. Chaturvaktra Ganapati

I contemplate on Brahman caturmukha (four faced) Ganesha who holds lotus, staff, water-pot used by ascetics, axe, lasso, rosary, elephant goad and book in eight hands, he has a golden radiance and a big belly, he has four elephant faces and wears a yellow cloth, and accompanied by three shaktis.


Long time ago, lord brahma the creator, became proud of himself, struck by ego, he did not remember the lord ganesha. He assumed himself to be the greatest lord of the worlds., obstructed by vighna (impediments) he lost his power of creation, and the power of realizing things from his thoughts. He became equal to any other ordinary soul, though he had the stature of the creator. Then he left his ego and started meditating through yoga. He chanted the sacred one syllable mantra of ganesha and penanced and remained in austerity and meditated continuously without gap for twelve years Of his life.

A great light developed from his heart centre, that light emerged outside through his four faces and appeared before him taking the form of ganesha with four elephant heads. Lord chaturmukha (four headed brahma) then worshipped the four headed ganesha and attained many great boons. He also got back his lost powers of creation and realizing everything he thought of.

This is why (to bless lord brahma) lord ganesha took the four elephant headed form.

Chaturvaktra Ganapati is the giver of all kinds of (manifold) growth and development.
