50. Shanmukha Ganesha

I bow to the shanmukha (six faced) ganesha who has six elephant faces. Has the crescent on his crown, holds lasso, elephant goad, rooster, Shakti weapon, Vajra weapon, bow and arrow, gesture of wish giving and protection, trident, sword, shield, and mace. He has the complexion of a newly
blossomed bandhujivaka (Pentapetes Phoenicea) flower, he has three eyes, has a big belly. He has a crown with shining gems embellished on it.

After killing the simhavaktra (lion faced demon) lord sadanana (the six faced lord), surapadma then became very angry, and brought the demons from various brahmandas (universes), due to lack of space, they stood in the other worlds fully ready and eager to fight.

On seeing that, the kind skanda understood the desire of the demon warriors, he worshiped lord Shiva with great devotion.

Lord Shiva initiated the six syllabled mantra of lord Gunesha to skanda with love. Guha (skanda) meditated on lord ganesha and chanted the mantra with pure devotion for Ganesha.

Ganesha residing in the heart centre in the form of divine supreme light (param jyoti) came out through all the six faces and appeared before him in the form of six faced ganesha who had
six elephant faces, Skanda - the son of shankara worshipped Gunesha and attained the celestial weapon ganapa astra.

With the help of that celestial weapon, lord skanda killed all the demons who fought against him from different worlds.

This Shanmukha Ganesha is the atman (soul) of Skanda.
