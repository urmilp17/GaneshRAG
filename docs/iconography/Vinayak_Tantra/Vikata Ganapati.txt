16. Vikata Ganapati

Vikata holds a garland, modaka sweet, lotus and axe in his lotus like hands. He is adorned with a garland of pearls in his neck. He bears the moon on his crest. He has three eyes and a big belly. He is red in color and is ac companied by the goddesses jivada (life giving power) and pushti (goddess of strength). He lord wears a celestial gem named chinatmani (the gem which has the power of fulfilling all the desires which we think of) I contemplate on the Ganapati who is known as Vikata and is adorned with a luminous and divine dress.

Lord ganesha is the form of braham described as sat, ganesha in the form of lord surya. (sun god) is vikata. This is the form of lord ganesha who rectifies all the diseases and illness and blesses with life and energy.
