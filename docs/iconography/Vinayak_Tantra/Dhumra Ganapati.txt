40. Dhumra Ganapati

The lord is mounted on a fine swift horse and is adorned with a beautiful yellow silk garment, has an huge and strong body which is dark like a water filled cloud. He has sword and shield in his hands, I worship that lord Dhumra ganapati who has a human face.

When the dharma of varnas and asrama (four stages of life) in the era of kali even in the country of bharat (present day India) the lord who will be incarnating on earth along with lord kalki the incarnation of Vishnu and destroy the evil people who have lost the Vedic culture on earth and establish the dharma along with the varna and ashrama system (in a proper manner)
