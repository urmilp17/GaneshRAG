119. Navaneeta Ganapati

The lord has a smiling face, his body has the complexion equal to butter (creamy white), he holds the lasso, pestle, has a big modaka sweet in his trunk. He is adorned with a white garment. He has elephant face. He is the the lord navanita who is birthless he is pleased by being worshiped with camphor, I contemplate and meditate on him who is accompanied by his wives navanita vani and navanita laksmi.

The gods consumed the butter, which was born from the milky ocean and the nectar, which stops death and became free of aging and death. Then in the course of time, two demons were born by the name jara and mruti. By the power of the boons granted to them. They terrorized and afflicted the worlds, the gods and humans were afflicted and brought into great and deep sufferings by them.

The gods led by lord Vishnu created an idol of ganesha made of butter and invoked and worshiped Ganesha in it. Lord Ganesha emerged from the butter idol, accepted vani the daughter of brahma, and kamala the daughter of Vishnu in marriage ritualistically and killed the wicked demons.

The lord always shows his presence in dakshinavarta.
