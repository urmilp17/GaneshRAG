19. Yoganidra Ganesha

I worship him whose form is totally in the color of the dawn sky similar to the color of Pentapetes phoenicea, he has two tusks, he holds lasso, elephant goad, a garland, in his hands, he is seated on a great white lotus in yogic sleep, the ganesha who can be attained only through yoga, the lord is accompanied by the goddesses buddhisarasvati and Sriyogasiddhi.

Ganesha the embodiment of yoga who is in the state of yogic sleep is refered to as svanandesa in the vedas.

Yoganidra Ganesha 15 the giver of peace of mind, calmness and accomplishments in the path of yoga. (and yogic powers)
