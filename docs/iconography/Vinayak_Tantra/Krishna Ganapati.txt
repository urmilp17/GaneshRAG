68. Krishna Ganapati

He holds conch, discus, tusk and modaka sweet with his hands. He is in the color of the dark rain filled clouds. He wears the kaustubha ornament and wears the yellow silk cloth. He is the one who blessed Radhakrishna with boons. He has three eyes, he is accompanied with the goddesses vidya (goddess of knowledge and wisdom) and vibhtti (the goddess of powers) I meditate on that lord krishna ganapati who is elephant faced and has a moon on his crest as his ornament.

The lord ganesha who emerged from the meditation of Radhakrishna is called
krsnavighnesvara as established in vedas and other sacred scriptures. He is the artman of Radhakrishna
