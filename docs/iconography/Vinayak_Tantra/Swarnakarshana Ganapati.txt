5. Swarnakarshana Ganapati

I contemplate on the lord who lives in the golden house (palace), he who is in golden color and has extraordinary eyes, (has three eyes) and holds lasso, elephant goad, and shows the gesture of wish giving and the gesture of protection in his four hands, accompanied by Vasudha (who is giver of wealth) and Shri (Goddess who is embodiment of wealth)

He is worshipped for obtaining gold and wealth. He 1s praised as the giver of gold.
