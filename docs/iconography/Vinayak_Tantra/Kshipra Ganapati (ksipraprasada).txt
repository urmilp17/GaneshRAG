60. Kshipra Ganapati (ksipraprasadana)

The lord holds tusk, lasso, elephant goad and wish giving tree in four hands, a jar set with jewels in his trunk. He is reddish like Hibiscus rosa-sinensis (shoe flower) he wears a yellow cloth; 1 worship the kshipraGanesha who is elephant faced and has three eyes.

The gods, goddesses and sages once were arguing amongst themselves on who is the greatest among brahma, Vishnu, eesha, Shakti, dinesha (the lord of the day - son god) and ganesha.

In the same time, vighna arrived in the form of a mighty and terrific elephant. On seeing the elephant, the gods (indra and others) and sages praised their favorite gods) some praised shiva and some prayed to vishnu, some prayed to surya the sun god and some other
prayed to Shakti and some of them praised ganesha and requested him to save them.

The lord ganesha the protector of the devotees, instantaneously appeared. On the sight of lord ganesha, the great vighna disappeared. Lord ganesha saved everyone (instantaneously) and blessed his devotees by giving them what they desired for and the king of the ghanas disappeared.

The lord who got quickly pleased and appeared before his devotees is called “kshipraprasadana” he who can be pleased quickly, with less efforts) he is also known as kshipra Ganapati (the quick ganesha).
