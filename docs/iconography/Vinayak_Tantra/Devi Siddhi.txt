I meditate upon the supreme Goddess Siddhi, the divine consort of Lord Ganesha.
She is golden-hued, holding a golden lotus, with a smiling face, adorned in yellow garments. She stands on the left side of Lord Vighneśa (Ganesha), with her graceful left arm hanging naturally.
Her sacred feet are worshipped by devotees who bow with their heads and hair touching her sandals. She wears the crescent moon as an ornament and is adorned with a garland of kalhāra (red water lilies).
She is beautified with the precious Cintāmaṇi jewel as her ornament. She possesses three eyes, signifying her divine omniscience. Upon that supreme Goddess Siddhi, I meditate with devotion.
