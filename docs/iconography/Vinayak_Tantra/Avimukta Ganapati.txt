113. Avimukta Ganapati

He is in the color of the pearl. Has an elephant face, wears the moon on his crest and has three eyes, he is the lord of final liberation, he shows the gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection, holds elephant goad and lasso in his hands. He is accompanied by mokshasiddhi (goddess of attainment of liberation) and moksabuddhi (goddess of wisdom of liberation) . I always meditate on lord Avimukta Ganesha who has both the tusks on his face.

Avimuktaganesa manifested from the meditation of vishvesa (lord vishwanatha), he is the giver of final liberation.
