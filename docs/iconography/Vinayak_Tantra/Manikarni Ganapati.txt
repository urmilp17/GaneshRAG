107. Manikarni Ganapati

In his lotus like hands, he holds lotus, water lily, blue lotus and modaka sweet (in his trunk), and garland, He wears wonderful ornaments which are set with various precious gems. He has a pure white lustre. Wears the cresent moon on his crest and has a great elephant face. He has his consorts punyada (she who gives merit and virtue) and papahantri she who destroys sins) to his sides. I worship that karniganesha in my heart.

Turiya ganga (the fourth ganga) is known as manikarni in the Vedas. The lord who blessed manikarni with various boons is manikarniganesvara. He is the destroyer of all sins and giver of great merits and virtue.
