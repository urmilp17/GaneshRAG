22. Ganadhipa

He is red in complexion, is adorned by a red cloth, has a great elephant face and
three eyes in it, he holds lasso and elephant goad and shows the wish giving gesture and gesture of complete protection, he also holds a piece of his tusk. Bears moon on his crest. He is the lord of the gaNas. He is the foundation of the entire universe he is adorned with love by his loved consorts and beloved devotees, he rides a lion, has a big belly he is the father (creator) of all leaders of the gaNa armies. I bow in reverence to the lord of the impediments.

The lord who has assumed a form and shows certain attributes is ganadhipa. He is the giver of all kinds of happiness and the destroyer of all kinds of dangers and sorrows.
