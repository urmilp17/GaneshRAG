82. Varada Ganapati

I contemplate on the varada Ganapati who is accompanied by his con-
sorts varada vani and varada Lakshmi, who has pomegranate, tusk, club, lotus, bow, blue lotus, trident, lasso, discus, and a golden pot filled with precious gems in his hands, who is red in color, has lion as his mount, has an elephant face, wears yellow silk garment and has a big belly.

The lord ganesha who blessed the deity of suklapaksacaturthi with various boons is called varada Ganapati. He is the one who fulfilled the wishes of the seer grtsama. Varada Ganapati is the giver of worldly pleasures and final liberation.
