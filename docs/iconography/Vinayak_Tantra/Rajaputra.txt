38. Rajaputra

The lord rides a horse, has three eyes, he has the handsomeness and splendor of hundred manmathas (the god of lust) he has radiant eyes, he is adorned by bracelets made of precious gems, with lots of precious stones, he is in color of precious stones, bears a crown in his head made of nine precious gems.

He is elegant and handsome, he is always with rajavidya (goddess of kingly wisdom) and rajalaksmi (goddess ofkingly wealth)


Lord Ganesha manifested as the son of king soma to destroy demons led by
matta and pramatta. He is known as Rajaputra Ganapati.

Rajaputra is the lord who blesses his devotees with the royal wealth. (wealth of
the kingdom)
