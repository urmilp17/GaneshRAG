53. Shulatanka Ganapati

He is of black complexion, he has the cresent on his crest, he has four hands holding trident, axe, lasso, and his tusk, he has three eyes. He has a bracelet which is embellished with gems, he is adorned with a great crown and necklace. 1 worship that lord of the ganas known by the name shulatamka.

Lord ksetrapala bhairava who severed the head of lord brahma the creator was affected by the sin of brahmahatya. He pleased Ganesha through penance. Lord Ganesha emerged from bhairava’s
heart lotus and purified him from that sin. The form in which ganesha appeared to purify bhairava is shulatanka ganesa

Shulatanka Ganapati is the destroyer of all great sins he is the one who purifies us from all the sins commited by us unknowingly and knowingly.
