28. Sarvavighnanashana

The lord holds lasso and elephant goad, and shows the gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection, holds a great axe and a lotus in his six hands. He is the one who blessed vidhata (brahma) he is the elephant lord. He has the skin tone of bandhujivaka (Pentapetes Phoenicea) flower. He is accompanied by the goddesses sarvavighnavinasini (she who destroys all obstacles) and
sarvasiddhividhayini (she who blesses with all success and powers).

Vinayaka tantra describes the incident in detail in patala 66 which describing the form of ‘sarvavighna nashana’.

Brahma lost his power of creation affected by obstacles, then he remained in austerity and did penance, siddhi (the goddess of success) and buddhi (the goddess of wisdom) were born as the outcome of his austerity. Lord ganesha took siddhi and buddhi in marriage. The lord appeared
before brahma, cleared all the obstacles and impediments and blessed him with the power of creation. The form in which lord ganesha manifested before brahma the creator, is called
sarvavighnanaSsana by the wise.
