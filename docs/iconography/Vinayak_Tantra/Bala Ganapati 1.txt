55. Bala Ganapati

The lord is as bright as ten thousand suns rising simultaneously, holds a modaka sweet in his trunk, he holds mango, sugarcane, banana and jackfruit in his hands. The lord wears many chains made of precious gems, wears jingling anklets and bracelets, I contemplate on balaganadhipa who has the splendor of crores of manmathas in my heart centre.

Bala Ganapati is the son of gayatri the wife of surya (sun god). He is the one who lies in the big banyan leaf in the beginning of creation; he is the one who initiated brahma the creator with the ekaksara mantra (one syllable mantra) of ganesha.

Bala Ganapati is the lord who blesses his devotees with sons ,daughters, grand daughters and grandsons.
