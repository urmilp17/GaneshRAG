116. Lakshavighneshwara

He is radiant as many bright suns, has elephant face, adorned with fragrant pastes, has lasso, garland, elephant goad and axe in his hands, has three eyes, he shines with various decorations (ornaments and garlands) has the crescent on (as) his crown and is accompanied by laksasiddhi and laksabuddhi and has the peacock as his mount. I bow to that lakshavighnesha.

There is a Ghana (attendant of ganesha) named laksha. He is a partial form (amshavatara) of Ganesha. In svananda loka (abode of ganesha), Laksha manifested as the son of goddess buddhi, and when lord ganesha incarnated, laksha was born as the son of goddess siddhi. Laksha vighneSvara is the form
of lord ganesha who was worshiped by laksha. He is the giver of boons to laksha.

Laksha Ganapati is the giver of all kinds of wealth.
