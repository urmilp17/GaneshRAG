61. Siddhi Vinayaka 

He who embraces the deities of eight siddhis (great powers) he has four hands, he bears a moon on his crest. He is also embraced by the eight siddhis one who has his trunk on the pot like breasts of prapti and other siddhis. He is adorned with red clothes and he is red in complexion. He is the one who was worshipped by Brahma (the creator) and he is the one who blessed Brahma (the creator) with all his deSires.

Siddhi vinayaka manifested and took the eight great power goddesses who were the daughters of brahma the creator in marriages

Siddhi Vinayaka is the one who blesses us with eight great yogic powers
