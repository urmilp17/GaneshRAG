17. Vighnaraja

I worship the lord vighnaraja (the king and controller of all obstacles (personified) who has the face of a great elephant, who is holding conch, discus, elephant goad, flowery arrows, lasso, sugarcane bow, axe and saliguccham (a cluster of shali grains) in his lotus like hands and always mounted on the big rat, he who has reddish hue, is wearing the moon on his crest, adorned with three eyes, he who is accompanied by siddhalakshmi and vagisvari.

Vighnaraja is the destroyer of all obstacles and imdepements.
