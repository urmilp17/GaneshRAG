96. Vamana Ganapati

I contemplate on the lord vamana Ganapati who is accompanied by the goddesses anurupa (the godesss who is most subtle and minute) and mahadrupa (the goddess who is the deity of largeness and hugeness) and he is holding discus, lotus, elephant goad, lasso, conch, axe, tusk, sword, bow, mace in his ten hands. He has peacock as his mount, he bears the moon as his crestal ornament, he has a garland in his trunk. He has a short neck, elephant face, he has long twisted locks of hair, and he is handsome and has golden hue and has a big belly.

vamana ganesha is the one who blessed lord vamana and gave him boons.
