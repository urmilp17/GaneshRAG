35. Dundheesha Vinayaka

He has elephant face, bears a moon on his crest, has three eyes; he has a broken tusk, arrow, trident, sudarshana discus, knife, axe, lasso, elephant goad, bow, sword and shield. He is in the color of shoe flower (hibiscus), one who was born from the anger of goddess gauri, He rides a lion i worship that dhundhiraja.

He is the lord who was born from the anger of goddess Shakti for destroying the demon durasada. He is the one who gave back kashi to Vishvesha. He is known as dhundhiraja. He is the leader of kashi city.

dundheesha is the giver of all siddhis and the destroyer of all obstacles.
