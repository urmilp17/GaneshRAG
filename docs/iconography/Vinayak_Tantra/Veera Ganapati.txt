58. Veera Ganapati

Lord holds vétala, shakti weapon, bow, arrow, lasso, axe, discus, khatvanga (skull of toop of staff), spear, sword, snake, elephant goad, flag, big mace, mallet, and trident and shows the gesture of complete protection and wish giving in eighteen hands. Holds tusk on his trunk, he has three eyes, and he is called the viraganapati, accompanied by dhanurvidya and viralaksmi

He who was born from kala-agni-rudra and the enemy of the demons is Vira Ganapati.
He is the lord who blesses us with courage, valor.
