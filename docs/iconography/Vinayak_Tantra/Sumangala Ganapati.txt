72. Sumangala Ganapati

He has sugarcane bow, five flowery arrows, lasso and elephant goad in his
four hands, and he is the beloved son of sumangali. He is elephant faced and handsome. He is red in color he wears a crescent moon as his ornament and has three eyes. He is with the goddesses ksemankari (she who does the welfare to all beings) and mangala (she who is the embodiment of auspiciousness) I contemplate that sumangalaganadhisa always in my mind.

There is a class of pitrdevas called shobhanas, the were born from brahma the creator, their Shakti (power) is goddess svadha. She is also known as sumangali lord ganesha who incarnated as her son 15 called sumangala Ganapati. He is the giver of auspiciousness and welfare.
