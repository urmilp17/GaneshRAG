99. Pingala Ganapati

He is equal to limitless gods of love (kandarpa) in beauty, he has three eyes and has a big belly, he is smiling, in his trunk he holds a modaka sweet made of white sesame seeds . he wears the moon on his crest. He has a ripe mango, axe, creaper of wish giving and sugarcane, he is accompanied by Sridevi (goddess of wealth) and samrddhi (goddess of abundance). I bow to and worship that elephant faced lord named pingala.

The form of Ganesha who emerged from the meditation and visualization
of lord hatakesa rudra. He is the one who fulfilled the wishes of lord shiva and (gave him many boons) and is called pingala Ganapati.

He is the one who blesses us with abundance of manifold wealth.
