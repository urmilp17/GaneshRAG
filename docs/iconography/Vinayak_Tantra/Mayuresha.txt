36. Mayuresha

Remember him who is named Mayuresha in your mind, the one he is holding lotus (with his trunk), lasso , elephant goad, axe and gesture of protection in his four hands, he is mounted on a peacock, he has crystal like complexion, he bears the moon on his crest, he has three eyes. He is the beloved son of sundarl. He is accompanied with goddess mahasiddhi and mahabuddhi. He wears the wish fulfilling gem (chinta ratna) as an ornament that is embellished with nine gems.

Lord Gunesha who incarnated as the son of goddess Shakti on earth to kill the demon Sindhu is mayuresa. Mayuresha is the form of ganesha who gives all the wealth and accomplishments.
