44. Dhundi Ganaka

The lord Dhundi ganaka is golden in color, he has a footwear made of precious gems, he has two eyes and a human face. He holds a great book and lotus in his hands. He has fragrant pastes applied on his body. He wears a silk cloth, has a girdle in his hip, he wears a holy mark on his forehead, he is handsome and is the devotee of ganesha, he is the lord of the siddhas, worship him who blesses us with all tattvas (highest spiritual truths) and he who gives us the wisdom of the three times - past, present and future.


Sage ganaka who was born from the body of dhundhiraja for deluding king Divodasa and to give back kasi to lord Shiva is dhundhiganaka.

This form of the lord ganesha is prayed for the knowledge of past present and future happenings (i.e powers of predictions)

This form of ganeshais the giver of wisdom of past, present and future.
