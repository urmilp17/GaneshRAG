Mahasiddhi

I worship Mahasiddhi, the Supreme Power (Para-Shakti) who encompasses the universe and resides on the left side of Ganesha. She shines with the brilliance of molten gold, wears divine garments and a beautiful bodice, and is endowed with eternal youth. She wears a garland of gems and white water lilies (Kalhara), is adorned with Chintamani ornaments, and has eyes overflowing with compassion. Her hands hold a lotus and gracefully display the gestures of boon-giving (Varada) and fearlessness (Abhiti/Abhaya).
