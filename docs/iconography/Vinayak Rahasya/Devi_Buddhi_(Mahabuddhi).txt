Mahabuddhi

I worship Mahabuddhi, the power of divine experience (Bhoga-Shakti) who resides on His right side. She possesses a pure crystal-like complexion, wears white and yellow garments with a yellow-dark bodice, and is brimming with blossoming youth. She wears a garland of golden Champaka flowers, is adorned with Chintamani ornaments, and bears a gentle smile on her lotus-like face. Her hands hold a lotus and display the gestures of boon-giving and fearlessness.
