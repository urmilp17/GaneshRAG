The Description of the State of the Uninitiated Ganapatya

Narada and the assembled sages posed a final, practical question to the Sun God, Surya, regarding the followers of Ganesha. Having just learned that initiated, single-minded devotees bypass the cosmic officials, the sages asked, "We understand how the formally initiated devotees are sustained. But there are many devoted Ganapatyas on earth who have not received formal spiritual initiation (Diksha). If they do not have that direct, royal decree, how do they attain success in acquiring physical nourishment and worldly enjoyment?"

Surya replied, clarifying the spiritual mechanics for the uninitiated. He explained that uninitiated Ganapatyas cannot bypass the cosmic bureaucracy. These Vedic, twice-born devotees must diligently perform the standard, traditional duties prescribed in the scriptures. By strictly observing the Shrauta (Vedic) and Smarta (traditional/Puranic) rites, they actively appease the subsidiary deities who govern cosmic actions and material delivery.

However, their devotion to the Supreme Lord does not go unnoticed. Because these devotees possess a genuine, heartfelt love for Ganesha and hold firmly to the spiritual identity of a Ganapatya, the subsidiary deities look upon them with special favor. Being fully satisfied by the devotees' meticulous adherence to ritual actions and karmic laws, these cosmic officials gladly and efficiently grant them their desired material fruits and worldly success.
