The Exposition of Shiva's Nature as the Mushak

Sanaka and others said:

Some declare Shankara to be the mouse; please state the reason for this!

Ganaka said:

The form composed of the four Brahmans is Gunesha, who is the fourth. When associated with Maya in the four states, he is called Parameshvara. The fourth state is the aggregate of the four, and is renowned as Turiya. In the lower part, the vehicle of the fifth, it is precisely the Turiya that is spoken of. The fifth is mounted upon the fourth; therefore, he is remembered as the mouse.

Thus concludes the Exposition of Shiva's Nature as the Mouse.
