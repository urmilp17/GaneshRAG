Description of the Satisfaction Arising from Pleasing Gunesh and Others

Narada and others said:

This fourfold universe is the body of the Lord of Prayer, Brahmanaspati. The individual souls, the presiding lords, and the various forms of the world are known to be made of His body. Tell us, by pleasing them, how much satisfaction is felt by Gunesh?

Surya said:

By the complete satisfaction of the Jivas and Ishvaras of the fourfold worlds, half of the total satisfaction of Gunesh is indeed attained, and not otherwise.

Gunesh, who consists of the three Gunas, is the Lord of these Jivas and Ishvaras; He is the Lord who exists as the collective form of all four worlds.

I, Surya, am said to be the bodily manifestation of the Supreme Self, Gunesh. By the thorough satisfaction of Gunesh, half of His satisfaction is fulfilled, referring to the internal or essential satisfaction.

The deities Shakti, Isha, Vishnu, and Bhaskara are essentially the limbs of Gunesh. The wise describe them as being composed of a quarter-portion of Gunesh.

The five Great Lords, each associated with a specific Guna and residing within the universe, are endowed with a fifth part of a quarter-portion, representing a further fractional manifestation.

The local gods, the ten guardians of the directions, and others are not direct portions. Rather, those gods and sages are said to be the bearers of His divine glory.

By pleasing Shiva and the others who reside in the Turiya, transcendental state, only an eighth portion of satisfaction is attained, and not otherwise. O best of the twice-born, the satisfaction of the Supreme corresponds exactly to the portion of the form being worshipped.

Thus ends the description of the satisfaction arising from pleasing Gunesh and others.
