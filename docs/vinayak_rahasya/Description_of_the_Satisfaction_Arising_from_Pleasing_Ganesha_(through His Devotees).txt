Description of the Satisfaction Arising from Pleasing Ganesha (through His Devotees)

Narada and others said:

Tell us, how is satisfaction attained by the Lord through the pleasing of the Ganapatyas, the followers of Ganesha?

Surya said:

By pleasing a Yogi, Ganesha—the Leader of Yoga—attains full satisfaction. Similarly, He attains complete satisfaction by the pleasing of one who has received the Nirvana-diksha, the highest initiation of liberation.

Ganesha, the Lord of Prayer, attains three-quarters satisfaction through certain advanced seekers, and He attains half satisfaction through the pleasing of one endowed with Yoga-diksha, initiation into the path of Yoga.

The Lord certainly attains half of that, meaning one quarter, by the pleasing of one initiated into Upasana, ritual worship. By pleasing one initiated into the Bhakti-marga, the path of devotion, the Lord attains half of that, meaning one eighth.

By pleasing uninitiated Ganapatyas, the Lord of Ganas is satisfied only to a minute degree, which is granted simply because they hold the internal sentiment or thought of Ganesha.

Thus ends the description of the satisfaction arising from pleasing Ganesha.
