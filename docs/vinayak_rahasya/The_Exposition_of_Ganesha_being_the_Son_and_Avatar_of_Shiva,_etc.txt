The Exposition of Ganesha Being the Son and Avatar of Shiva, etc.

Sanaka and others said:

Some say that Vighnesha is the son of the Lord of Parvati. Some say he is the son of Varanya, and some say he is the son of Kashyapa. But you say that Ganesha is the embodiment of the Supreme Brahman. This has created a doubt in us; O Guru, please tell us the truth!

Shiva said:

Brahman, which is of the nature of Knowledge and Bliss, is proclaimed as the essence of its own joy. That very Brahman appears in two forms: with attributes and without attributes. The Saguna form is said to be united with the Primordial Nature. It is for the protection of the devotees and for the destruction of the wicked. And that Brahman, by its own will and through its own Maya, assumes a form. When it takes on a manifested form in the universe, it is known in the scriptures as an Avatar.

O Sages! The name Gajanana is established in this world as an Avatar. He has been the son of me, of Kashyapa, and of Shakti, and so on. That Lord became a son through penance and for the establishment of Dharma. The Avatars are endless, as proclaimed in the Agamas. Among them, one hundred and eight are proclaimed as the main ones. Lord Gajanana is the deity who is the cause and purpose of the Avatars. Having disappeared from the world, he returns again; there is no doubt about this. Many times, Lord Ganesha has been born as my son. Through this, Gajanana has attained the fame of being the son of Shiva.

Thus concludes the Exposition of Ganesha Being the Son and Avatar of Shiva, Etc.
