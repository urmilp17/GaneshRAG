The Exposition of the Boon Given by Shiva, etc.

Narada and others said:

It is declared that Shiva, first of all, granted to Mayuresha, the avatar of Ganesha, the state of being worshipped by all. How can one who is endowed with a boon given by Shiva become superior to Shiva himself?

Surya said:

Pleased by the penance of both Shiva and Parvati, he became their son. And Shiva, who knows the ultimate truth, joyfully granted him the worship that precedes that of all other deities. And for the attainment of Self-knowledge by Gautama and other sages, out of paternal affection for his son, he granted him the primacy of being worshipped first by all. That which was given, Gajanana accepted out of devotion to his father. Just as Ramachandra accepted the kingdom given by his father, or as Hari does, O best of sages, this does not imply any inferiority on the part of Shiva.

Thus concludes the Exposition of the Boon Given by Shiva, Etc.
