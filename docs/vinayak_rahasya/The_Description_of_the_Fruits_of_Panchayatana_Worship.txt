The Description of the Fruits of Panchayatana Worship

Narada and the assembled sages approached the Sun God, Bhaskara, seeking to understand the ultimate spiritual destination of those who perform the geometric, five-fold worship. "O Bhaskara," they asked, "what is the final fruit and reward for a soul that perfectly executes the Panchayatana Puja?"

Surya replied, explaining the precise spiritual mechanics and the immense rewards of this practice. He detailed that a mortal dedicated to this five-fold worship (Pancharchana) must proceed sequentially through the cosmic gateways. The practitioner first honors Vishnu, then moves to Shambhu, then Shakti, and then Surya. Finally, with the peripheral energies harmonized, the practitioner worships Guneshvara, who is established firmly in the absolute center of the layout.

By executing this worship strictly according to the sacred rules, the devotee's internal consciousness undergoes a profound transformation. They first attain Chitta-shuddhi, the absolute purification of the mind. In this clarified state of consciousness, the true knowledge of the Absolute (Brahma-jnana) naturally dawns. Empowered by the perfection of this knowledge, the soul achieves Kaivalya Mukti—the ultimate liberation of absolute, non-dual isolation from material existence.

However, Surya noted that not all souls achieve instant liberation in their current lifetime. For those excellent practitioners who fall just short of immediate non-duality, a glorious alternative awaits. Upon leaving their physical bodies, they ascend to the supreme realm of Gunesh. Residing in that divine sphere, they continue to acquire ultimate knowledge. When the cosmic cycle (Kalpa) finally comes to an end, they, alongside Gunapa, attain Brahma-sayujya—complete and permanent union with Brahman, ensuring they are never again subject to the cycle of rebirth. Alternatively, the immense spiritual merit gathered from this five-fold worship can elevate a practitioner to the status of an exalted Ganapa (a divine attendant or enlightened master) similar to the great sage Bhrigu.

Surya then revealed the highest peak of this spiritual path. If a devotee receives formal Ganapatya initiation (Diksha) and dedicates themselves to the intense worship of Gajanana, the power of their ultimate liberation (Nirvana) catapults them into the "Fifth State"—the realm of Svananda.

Concluding his discourse, Surya described the nature of this ultimate destination. "O lords of sages," he declared, "all those who ascend to this Fifth state become the very embodiment of Brahman itself. In that supreme state of existence, the veil of duality is permanently shattered, and the illusion that separates the individual soul (Jiva) from the Supreme Lord (Ishvara) ceases to exist forever."
