The Recitation of the Names of the Five Deities Used in Panchayatana Worship

Sanaka and the other sages said:

O Master, please tell us the sacred names of the deities worshipped in the Panchayatana layout!

Ganaka said:

Vinayaka, Jyeshtharaja, Varada, Brahmanaspati, Ekadanta, Vakratunda, Dhundhisha, Vighnanayaka, Lambodara, Ganapati, Chintamani, Nijasthita, Mangalyesha, Sarvapujya, Vikata, Dharanidhara, Gajanana, Siddhibuddhipati, Ashaprapuraka, Mayuresha, and Heramba. Whoever recites or listens to these twenty-one divine names of the great-souled Ganesha becomes completely freed from all obstacles and attains all perfections.

Mahalakshmi, Mahasiddhi, Mulaprakriti, Avyaya, Omkararupini, Maya, Kamala, Sundari, Rama, Aja, Eka, Keshamata, Yoganandasvarupini, Vallabha, Brahmatanaya, Srishtisthityantakarini, Lakshmi, Brahmabhavasiddhi, Siddhi, Shri, Vighnanashini, Sahasradalapadmastha, Svanandalokavasini, Mukti, and Chintitada. Those foremost among Yogis who praise Mahasiddhi—the beloved Vallabha of the Supreme Self—with these twenty-five divine names attain all four goals of human life: Virtue, Wealth, Pleasure, and Liberation.

Vagishvari, Mahavidya, Mahabuddhi, Sarasvati, Dhi, Dharanavati, Medha, Brahmanandasvarupini, Panchachittavrittimayi, Brahmavidyapradayini, Brahmajnanamayi, Vani, Nijalokavasini, Prajna, Nirupadhimaya, Buddhi, Vidya, Virinchija, Vainayaki, Mahavani, Sharada, Vishvarupini, Sahasrarambujagata, Mati, and Devi. Those who praise the Sovereign Goddess Buddhi with these twenty-five sacred names obtain supreme knowledge and spiritual wisdom, experiencing both worldly enjoyment and ultimate liberation.

Keshava, Narayana, Madhava, Govinda, Vishnu, Madhusudana, Trivikrama, Vamana, Shridhara, Hrishikesha, Padmanabha, and Damodara. These are declared as the twelve divine names of Lord Vishnu, the consort of Rama.

Mahadeva, Maheshvara, Shankara, Vrishabhadhvaja, Shulapani, Kamanganashana, Devadevesha, Shrikantha, Hara, Parvatipati, Maharudra, and Shiva. These twelve sacred names are declared for Lord Shiva, the consort of Parvati.

Mitra, Ravi, Surya, Bhanu, Khaga, Pusha, Hiranmaya, Marichi, Savita, Aditya, Arka, and Bhaskara. These twelve divine names are declared for the Sun God, Bhaskara.

Simhasana, Shri Lalita, Maharani, Varankusha, Chapini, Tripura, Mahatripurasundari, Sundari, Chakranatha, Samrajni, Chakrini, Chakreshvari, Mahadevi, Kameshvari, Parameshvari, Kamarajapriya, Kamakotika, Chakravartini, Mahavidya, Shivanangavallabha, Sarvapatala, Kulanatha, Amnayanatha, Sarvamnayadhivasini, and Shringaranayika. Those who praise the Supreme Goddess with these twenty-five names attain the fulfillment of all their heart's desires.

Thus ends the Recitation of the Names of the Five Deities Used in Panchayatana Worship.
