The noble Upanisad of the lord of Vallabha
The pacification (mantra) is: om may it (— that which is to be studied)
protect both of us together. (May it help us both to enjoy. May we both be
successful. May that which has been studied by us be effective. May we not
hate each other. Om, peace, peace, peace.)
Now Kdasyapa asked the noble Marici:
Who is Vallabhé? Who is the lord of Vallabha?
Marici said:
Vallabha is the mistress of all people, the Sakti of brahman 15, the wife of
Vindyaka, the maya, Siddhalaksmi. Her husband is the highest self, the lord
of Vallabha. ॥
Why is he (called) Ganapati?

Ganapati is the lord-of the classes (gana): of Brahma, Visnu, Rudra and
others, of the brahmans (in the form of sheaths, viz.) anna etc.'®, of the
entities liké mahat |’.

Why is he (called) the elephant-faced one?

, The maya has the form of a man, the purusa the form of an elephant.

The clephant-faced one is the one who has the united form of prakrti and
purusa.

Why does he have ten arms?

From the principle of existence '* the subtle clements were born. The
worlds are (i.e. the worlds consist of) the group of subtle elements which

॥ consists of sound, touch, colour, taste, smell, (and) the totality of bodies of
(three types:) gross, subtle and causal, which are an aggregation of the

| subtle elements with their respective qualitics. Therefore the worlds are
constituted by the five elements. Jagar is (the same as) the world constituted
by triads. Having created it, having entered it, he became endowed with
form and without form!9. By a combination of the two (is manifested) the
lord of Vallabha who assumes a single, undivided form consisting of the .
true brahman and‘the brahman as the world. Below the neck he consists

of the world. (His) head is the fourth (i.e. transcendental) brahman. A
combination of the two (= torso and head) is the fifth brahman 20. The fifth
(brahman) entered this (world), manifest and unmanifest, and became the
inner controller. He created from the sky the lord of Pusti?', from the wind
and the mind the lord of Rati (= Kama), from the fire the lord of Girija
(= Siva), from the water the lord of Ramé (= Visnu), from the food the
lord of Bhiimi (न= Varaha), along with (their) consorts. He has ten arms
because he has thé undifferentiated form of the five with (their) consorts.
