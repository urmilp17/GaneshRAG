73. Chandana Ganapati

The lord rides a musk deer, his skin color is of red sandal, he holds water lily, blue lotus, lotus, sugarcane and bow and wears a red garland in neck and trunk, has the moon on his crown, has three eyes, and is very handsome. I contemplate on him - chandana ganapati who is together with tilakavani and gandhalaksmi.

bhargavi the incarnation of goddess Lakshmi worshipped ganesha with devotion by invoking him on sandal with the wish to attain lord Vishnu as her husband. Pleased by that deed of bhargavi, lord Vishnu decided to accept her, at that time, a demon named durbhaga kidnapped bhargavi with his evil powers of delusion and took her to his place. Greatly saddened by that, bhargavi remembered ganesha with devotion. A female attantend of ganesha appeared and took her and placed her in the presence of Vishnu, in the altar of marriage.

According to bhargavi advice, Vishnu along with bhargavi worshiped ganesha
with devotion by invoking him in sandal.

Lord Ganesha appeared before her from that sandal. By his order and permission, lord Vishnu married bhargavi in the presence of chandana ganesha. Demon durbhaga arrived there to fight, lord ganesha playfully killed him in war and blessed Vishnu and other gods and disappeared from there.

Vishnu, Lakshmi and other happily granted sandal many boons.

Chandana Ganapati is the destroyer of ill luck and misfortune.
