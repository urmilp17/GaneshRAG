90. Santana Ganapati

Lord shows the gesture of protection, and in that same hand he has his tusk, and in the hand showing the gesture of wish giving he has blue lotus, then he has lasso and elephant goad in his two lower hands, he wears the moon on his crest, he is the birthless god who has elephant face, he is touching the breast of his consort with his trunk. He is golden in complexion and handsome as ten million manmathas together. he is adorned with yellow silk. I worship the samtana Ganapati (child giving ganesha) accompanied with samtanalaksmi (goddess of childbirth) in my heart.

The lord ganesha appeared from under the samtana tree and rectified the effect
of mother parvati’s curse (of not being able to give birth to children through
their wives) for the gods. Hence, he is called samtanagananatha. By his grace, the gods attained children (sons and daughters) through their respective wives) in order.

Santana, Mandara, kalpaka, parijata and harichandana are classes of wish granting trees of heaven. He is the lord who rectifies the ill effects and obstacles relation to childbirth and blesses the devotees with offsprings.
