24. Panchadeva Janaka

I worship the lord maytresvara Ganapati who has elephant face, he who blesses us with everything, who is the creator of all five great gods (brahma, Vishnu, shiva, surya and shakti) and he who fulfills their wishes pleased by their sincere devotion and austerity. He has the golden hue, has his tusk, shows gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection, and holds a lasso. He has goddess siddhi and buddhi on his laps (on both sides) one of his tusks is broken (which he holds in one of his hands as already mentioned)

Lord Ganesha is worshiped in this form to make all gods favorable to devotees.
