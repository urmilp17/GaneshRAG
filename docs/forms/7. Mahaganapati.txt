7. Mahaganapati

Mahaganapati who is holding pomegranate, mace, sugarcane bow, trident, disc, lotus, lasso, blue lotus, grains, and tusk in ten hands and a jewel pot with trunk is to be contemplated as embraced by vallabha - his consort who holds a lotus in her hand and is adorned by various shiny ornaments. The vighnesha (mahaganapati) is the creator, destroyer and protector of universes and is the giver or all that we desire.

This is the form of Ganapati worshipped by lord kameshwara and god dess Lalita. 
The episode of how he appeared is well described in lalitopakhyanam (of brah manda purana) 
Vallabheshopanishad describes his mantra and worship.
