52. Simhavaktra Ganapati

I worship to the lord ganesha who is red in complexion like the Hibiscus flowers, he who holds the discus with thousand spokes, trident, lasso and elephant goad in his four hands. He is seated in the posture of virasana, he has three eyes and is wearing the crescent moon on his head and is having lion face with sharp fang like teeth.

To protect his devotee, the greatest of the demons, - prahlada, lord Vishnu assumed the form of man lion and emerged from the huge pillar of hiranyakashipu’s court.

To kill the evil hiranyakashipu, lord narasimha started meditating on lord ganesha, due to lord narasimha’s meditation, lord ganesha manifested with lion face, this form is called simhavaktra ganesha. He is the destroyer of all enemies.

On another occasion, lord ganesha manifestion in the lion faced ganesha form to protect a king - the king of harivarsha kingdom, who was a devotee of narasimha from. Lord simhavaktra ganesha killed many demons and protected the king of harivarsha playfully.
