23. Beeja Vighnesha

He is elephant faced and wearing the crescent moon on his crest and is seated on a throne embellished with gems. He is red in color he has lasso, elephant goad, shes the gesture of protection, gesture of wish giving, he has his tusk, pomegranate fruit in his lotus hands. He has a big round belly, is adorned with yellow silk, contemplate and visualize the lord who is known as bija Ganapati along with his consorts bijavani and bijarama in my heart centre.

This ganesha is the seed of three types of worlds. He 15 the one who killed matsarasura playfully. He is the Brahman who is in the form of the dot (bindu)

He who is the seed of all the three worlds he who is beyond and greater than the unmanifest (avyakta) is the purusha - guneSvara - the lord of gunas. He is bija vighnesa. - The embodiment of the sound of the one sacred syllable.

The one syllable mantra of OM is brahma (supreme reality). The lord bijavighnapa is the form (embodiment of OM).
