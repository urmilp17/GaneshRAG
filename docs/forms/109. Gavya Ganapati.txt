109. Gavya Ganapati

He is elephant faced and is black in color. He has a cup of curd and a cup of milk in his upper two hands, and has ladduka (sweet) and wish giving creeper in his lower two hands, he holds a modaka sweet with his trunk, he has a white wish giving cow as his mount, he has three eyes. He is accompanied by his consorts bahyashuddhi (goddess of external purification) and antasshuddhi (goddess of inner purification) I adore him - the gavya Ganapati in my heart.

Once goddess parvati (the eternal consort of lord Shiva) attained the form of a cow because of Shiva’s curse. Goddess who was in the form of cow, invoked ganesha in materials produced be the cow, worshipped ganesha with materials obtained from the cow. (i.e) the materials she produced in her cow form. (Milk, cow dung etc). Lord Ganesha emerged from those materials to bless goddess parvati. The form in which lord ganesha kindly appeared before goddess parvati is gavyaganesvara. Gavya - Ganesvara is the giver of internal and external purity and destroyer of all diseases.
