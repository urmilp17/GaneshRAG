111. Kapila Ganapati

The lord is elephant faced. He has a rosary, modaka, lotus and axe in his
hands, he has golden radiance, he bears a moon in his crown, he has 8 skin tone of a black cloud, and he is accompanied with yogasiddhi and yogavidya. He is named kapila ganapati and wears a chintamani (wish giving gem).

The lord ganesha emerged from the yagya kunda (sacred fire sacrifice) of
sage kapila, killed the ghanasura in war, and presented the great cintamani -
wish fulfilling precious gem to sage kapila. Sage kapila offered it to ganesha
by happily tieing the gem on lord ganesha's neck. That form of Ganapati is praised as chintamani Ganapati.

After that, kapila the great yogi, knowing, experiencing that Brahman is the prime cause of everything. He remained in the state of asampragyatasamadhi experiencing the extent of wisdom. By the effect of his asampragyatasamadh a great light (of atman) emerged from his heart centre. That light manifested as lord ganesha who is the atman of sage kapila. He is known as kapila ganesha.

He is the giver of yogis powers and yogic accomplishments.
