103. Kapardi Ganapati

He holds lotus, axe, lasso and elephant goad in four of his hands, has
a big belly, a pot of amruta adores his trunk. He bears a moon on his crest, is very bright white in color. He has gangasarasvati is his lap. He is elephant faced, has long twisted locks of hair, I contemplate on kapardiganapa.

Mahavani is the daughter of goddess ganga. Lord Ganesha took her hand in marriage. That form of ganesha is called kapardi ganapati. Kapardi Ganapati is worshiped for the destruction and rectification of one’s sins.
