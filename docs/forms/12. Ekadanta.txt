12. Ekadanta

I bow to Ekadanta (single tusked lord) in reverence, who is red in complex ion, is adorned by a red cloth, has three eyes, and bears a piece of moon on his crest. He holds lasso and elephant goad and shows the wish giving gesture and gesture of complete protection, he also holds a piece of his tusk. He is accompanied with his consorts mahavidya and mahamaya and rides a big mouse and has a big belly, one who is worshipped by Brahma the creator and other gods. 


Ekadanta Ganapati is the manifestation of brahman indicated by the statement SoHam -I am he, in the vedas. He is the killer of demon Mada (symbol of pride), he is the embdoment of ultimate wisdom. The lord who is not actually bound by attributes and he who is beyond the three gunas (modes of nature) by his near nature.

He is prayed for understanding and experience ones true self (the existence), he is also prayed for strength.
