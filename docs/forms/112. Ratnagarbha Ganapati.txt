112. Ratnagarbha Ganapati

I contemplate on the lord who has elephant face and whose body is made of nine precious gems, has three eyes and big belly, wears the wish granting gem as his ornament. He holds the nine gems with nine hands (eight hands and a trunk) bears a moon on his crest, he is young, and he is always together with dhanarthalaksmi (goddess of wealth) and dhanarthavidya. (goddess of wealth making wisdom) I contemplate on them in my heart centre.

Ratnagarbha is the form of Ganapati worshiped by lord Shiva. 

Ratnagarbha Ganapati is the giver of precious gems, magical powers, wealth, fulfilment of all wishes.
