74. Durmukha Ganapati

He who has his right knee on the back of shambu who has been pushed to the ground, With his two upper hands, he is holding the trident which is pierced on shambhus head and holding lasso and elephant goad with other two hands. He is having goddess chittavrttimayl and goddess chittaharsavisadada to his sides. Worship Durmukha Ganapati who has four hands.

On behalf of his demon devotee, once lord shiva hit the sun god. The consort of parvati made the orbit of the sun fall down. Parvati. The sun god the controller of time became angry, fought back with shankara. (Shiva) vishnu (surya Narayana) and shankara fought along fight.

Lord shiva roared after defeating Surya the sun god. Lord Ganesha came to know that lord shiva defeated his devotee (surya) and he caught hold of shiva and pushed him towards the ground playfully. (effortlessly)

He cut off shiva’s body. This form of ganesha is called durmukha. Pleased by the worship and prayers of goddess parvati ganesha spared lord shiva's life.

He destroys the evil forces of all the worlds with his anger.
