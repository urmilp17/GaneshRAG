78. Bhoomi Vighnesha

He is holding a golden vessel filled with food (rice), tusk, rice grains and pomegranate in his four hands. He is handsome with the handsomeness of millions of manmathas (god of lust and love) put together and has an elephant face, big belly, he has a golden pot embellished with nine precious gems in his trunk. He is adorned with yellow silk. I meditate on bhumivinayaka who has moon as an ornament on his crest and is accompanied by annalaksmi (the goddess of food) in my mind.


Annalakshmi who is also known as pushtilakshmi is the daughter of dharalaksmi. She is the one who nurtures and nourishes the worlds. The lord and eternal consort of Annalakshmi is bhimivighnesvara. This form of ganesha was worshiped by dhara (goddess earth)

He is the giver of desired food.
