108. Sthooladanta Ganapati

The lord has blue radiance, bears a moon in crest, he is as handsome as hundreds of manmathas, is elephant faced, has three eyes, has a garland of flowers in his trunk, he holds axe, lotus, conch and discus in his hands, he has two large tusks decorated with nine kinds of precious gems, the lord is together with goddesses vagbhuti (wealth of words and speech) and visvabhuti. (universal wealth)

The lord narayana residing in the turlyabhavana (the fourth abode)
which is beyond the abodes of brahma, Vishnu and rudra pleased lord Ganesha with his auterity and penance to gain to power to create brahma and others. The lord ganesha who appeared as the result of meditation of narayana is sthuladantaganesvara. sthuladantaganesvara is the giver of glory and fame.
