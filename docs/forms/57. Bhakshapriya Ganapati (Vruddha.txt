57. Bhakshapriya Ganapati (Vruddha Ganapati)

bhakshapriya ganapati holds coconut, banana, a sweet drink made with jaggery (payasa) and mango, he is moon white in color, he is elephant faced has three eyes and bears a moon on his crest. I worship the Vruddha Ganapati who is also called bhaksha Ganapati.

He is the son of sarasvati, adorned with all wealth, he is the son of hiranyapurusha. He took the form of a very elderly old man to test king janaka. He ate all the food of the kinds palace, then reached the home of trishira, a poor Brahmin of king janaka's kingdom, blessed him and showed him his true form. That form of ganesha is called bhakshapriya (ganesha who is fond of food), he is also known as vruddha Ganapati (old Ganapati)

The lord blesses his devotees with good tasty and healthy food.
