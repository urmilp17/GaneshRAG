93. Prachanda Ganapati

He holds bow, arrow, lasso, sword, shield, axe, lotus, elephant goad in his eight hands, wears a moon on his crest, has three eyes, wears a red garment and has a big belly, he has two shiny tusks which are decorated with nine precious gems and he is adorned by their luster, he has vermilion like hue, I bow to that prachanda ganapa who has an elephant face.


He who was born from the meditation of the noon sun is pracandavikrama
(pracanda Ganapati)

The one who blesses us with strength, prowess and victory.
