46. Dvimukha Ganapati

I think on the lord who has the a face of a ruttish elephant with color of a noon plant's flower, has a human face which is pure white in color, his body has the complexion on vermilion. He bears moons in his crest, has three eyes in each face, he holds trident, disc, lotus and tusk in his hands, a nice modaka sweet in his trunk, he wears red silk. I contemplate on that dvimukha ganapati (two faced ganapati).

To show that the supreme reality who assumed the form of elephant and human form is one and to establish that there is only one and not anything second to it (the non duality philosophy), the lord ganesha appeared before the sages who talk and preach duality (dvaita) with two faces. He is Dwimukha Ganapati.

Dvimukha Ganapati is the giver of both worldly materialistic pleasure and final liberation.
