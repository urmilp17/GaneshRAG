89. Ekobhayaprada Ganapati

He has a big modaka in his truck which is facing left. He has four hands, showing gesture of wish giving, holding tusk, lasso and elephant goad. His form has the lustre of the vermilion. He has elephant face and is wearing a red garment and has a bill belly. I bow to the ekobhayada Ganapati who 15 accompanied by bhukti( the goddess of worldly materialistic pleasures) and mukti (the goddess of final liberation).

He is the god who gives all success, accomplishments and yogic powers. He gives all worldly pleasures and also final liberation.
