41. Siddharupa Ganapati

The lord siddha has human face, wears a footwear made of precious gems, bears moon on his crest, holds the magical anjanam (black pigment or lotion or wash for eyes applied to the eyelashes or the inner coat of the eyelids as a cosmetic), yogic staff, holy ash, sandal paste in his hands. He wears kaupina (small piece of cloth worn over the privities), girdle, and a sacred mark on forehead. He has two eyes. He is handsome, is accompanied by sarvasiddhi (the embodiment of all kinds of powers) and sarvabuddhi. (embodiment of all kinds of knowledge)

When sage kapila propagated the philosophy of nirisvara sankhya - the philosophy of 25 principles of nature which did not mention isvara - supreme god, many sages and kings started following that principle and the vedic way of living made of three paths (karma duty, bhakti ~ devotion and gyana - wisdom) got destroyed and distracted because of nirisvara sankhya.

At that time, prayed and requested by the gods, lord ganesha assumed the form of a siddhayogi and showed the glory of his yogic powers and brought back those people (sages and kings) back to the vedic way of living which following the path of yoga.

Siddharupa Ganapati is the giver of vak siddhi and other siddhis (yogic powers).
