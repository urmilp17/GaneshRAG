101. Shweta Ganapati

Who is elephant faced, has the moon on his crest, has three eyes, is decorated with white garment, has amruta (the death stopping nectar of the gods) lotus, elephant goad and pestle, who is worshipped by even by lor Vishnu and other great gods, he who is the embodiment of deathlessness or the death stopping elixir, 1 worship the lord sveta gananayaka (white ganesha) who is accompanied by his consorts sweta kamala and Sveta vani.

While churning the milky ocean to obtain the amruta the nectar, which stops death, the gods were inflicted by obstacles and hindrances.

They (Vishnu and other gods) made an idol of ganesha with the foam of the milky ocean and worshipped him. Lord Ganesha appeared pleased by their worship. He is called svetaganapa by Vedas and sacred scriptures.

He is the form of ganesha who rectifies aging and death.
