110. Bala Ganapati

He is radiant like ten thousand rising suns, his face is of an elephant, he
holds a nice modaka sweet in his trunk, the lord holds mango, sugarcane, ba-
nana and jack fruit in his for hands, he wears wonderful chains and ornaments which has precious gems set on them. His anklets ring melodiously, I worship lord bala ganadhipa who is as handsome as ten million manmathas in my heart.

Lord Ganesha in child form who is always indulging child plays is balaganadhipa.
