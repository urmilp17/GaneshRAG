39. Kalanalaprashamana

The lord is surrounded by brahma, indra and other main gods, he wears the serpent king as his belly belt, which has precious gems shining in its head. The lord is pleased when worshipped with durva grass, he shows the gesture of Vara (Wish giving, and gesture of protection (abhaya mudra) he holds a lotus, he embraces the goddesses amruta amrtavani and amrtarama who are standing near him closely by his sides. The lord bears a moon on his crest, and he is yellow in color, has a human face. I remember the Lord Kalanalaprashamana (he who controlled kalanala) in my heart.

From the prowess of Dharmaraja (yama) A demon named kalanala (death fire) he became the oppressor of sages and gods, then when the lord ganesha was worshipped by the gods and sages, the lord took the form of a small boy and ate the low and worst demon kalanalasura. The lord Ganesha in this form is praised as Kalanalaprashamana.

The lord is the one who pacifies the fire of death and is the giver of the nectar which stops death. (the giver of immortality)
