4. Haridra Ganapati

I bow to the lord of the ganas (Ganapati) who is the dwelling place of com passion, who is holding lasso, elephant goad, sugarcane bow, arrow, lotus, tusk, shali (cluster of grains) , pot embellished with precious gems and is adorned with all ornaments and looks so pleasing. He has his two wives on both his sides, and is having a golden 
hue, he has yellowish complexion of the turmeric. He is one of the incarnations of vakratunda ganesha. He is the son of Shakti. He is the ganesha worshipped with the 
mahavidya bagalamukhi.
