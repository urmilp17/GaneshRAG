11. Sharada Ganapati

The lord holds criton and many weapons in his ten hands, (the weapons are as per the dhyana shloka of vallabha ganapati), he is white in complexion, he is embracing she who is pleased by listening to the Vedas, the daughter of the vedas she who blesses the Vedas with bliss, she wears moon as a crestal ornament, she is surrounded by méedha and other feminine goddesses who are embodiments of wisdom, who blesses us with various wisdoms. The goddess Sharada adores his left lap, one who blesses with all the desires of her worshippers, the lord enjoys the bliss of embracing her, and may that Sharadesha bless us with fortunes. 

The lord is prayed for knowledge, wisdom, intellect and skills.
