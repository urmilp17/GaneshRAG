97. Chaturdanta Ganapati

I contemplate on the Brahman caturdanta ganapati (four tusked ganapati) who holds a pot of amrta in his trunk, axe, lasso, elephant goad and lotus in hands, one who bears moon on his crest and has the radiance of crore moons, he is adorned and bright with four large tusks decorated with various precious gems, he has an attractive radiance which pleases our mind, he is accompanied by anagha and avyaya.

caturdantaganesvara is the lord who blessed airavata elephant with boons pleased by his worship.

He is the giver of royal wealth, elephants, horses, cows and cattle.
