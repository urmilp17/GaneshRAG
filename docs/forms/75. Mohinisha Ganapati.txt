75. Mohinisha Ganapati

I meditate on the lord who has gesture of wish giving, gesture of protection, lasso, and elephant goad in his hands. He is embraced by the goddesses Srisarvasakti who is holding a lotus in her hand and is seated on the left lap of the lord ganesha. She has a green hue, she is embranced by him with the hand which is showing the gesture of wish giving. The lord is very happy, has elephant face, he has the complexion of bandhuka flower.

Adisakti (the first and foremost power) mahamaya is known as sarvasakti (all power).

She emerged from the heart of goddess bhuvanesi as the result of her meditation and became her daughter.

The manifestation of all power - the daughter of goddess bhuvanesi was taken in marriage by lord ganesha, the lord of all. He is called as Shakti Ganapati, also known as mohinisha Ganapati.

This form of ganesha is the one who deludes all the worlds and can free his devotees from delusion.
