102. Amoda Ganapati

I worship the lord amoda who is elephant faced, holds elephant goad, lotus, lasso and shows gesture of wish giving in four hands, holds the modaka sweet in his trunk. He has sadanandavani as his consort and has nectarous radiance of the moon.

Amoda Ganapati is the embodiment of bliss, he is also known as Moda Ganapati.

He is worshipped for pleasing the hearts and minds of the angry ones. He can easily reduce their anger and make them be pleased on his devotee.
