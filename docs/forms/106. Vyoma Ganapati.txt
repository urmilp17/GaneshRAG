106. Vyoma Ganapati

A white water-lily, sugarcane bow, axe, flower arrows and lotus with his hands, wears crescent on his crest, he is elephant faced, he has the lustre of the moon and has a big belly. He has wonderful ornaments which are set with various precious gems and is adorned with a pure white garment. I contemplate on the lord vyomavighnesvara with his consort vyomasarasvati.

Ganesha who was worshiped by the deity of space (ether) is called vyomavighnesvara by those well versed in the Vedas.

Vyoma Ganapatis the form of Ganesha who is worshipped for attaining special power of telepathy, astral vision (foresight) etc.
