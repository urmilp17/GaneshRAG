69. Mitra Ganapati

He has the pleasant lustre which is equal to the hue of heated (refined) gold. He wears a yellow silk, he has a big belly, and he has four hands. He holds a big modaka sweet, his tusk and a pair of lotuses in his hands. He is with his consorts prajna (The goddess of wisdom, intelligence, knowledge) and kanti. (The goddess of brightness) He is adorned with different precious gems which are glowing and adding to his lustre. I worship he lord named Mitra Gananayaka who has elephant head with one broken tusk and (other unbroken).

Among the five gods who emerged from the body of Ganesha, mitra (the son god) is the hiranya purusha (the universal consciousness with the golden hue) he is the controller of time. The lord who emerged from the meditation of lord mitra is called mitra Ganapati. He is the atman of lord mitra (sun god)

Mitra Ganapati is the destroyer of all the diseases
