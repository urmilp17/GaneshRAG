26. Omkaara Ganesha

Omkara ganapati is as red as vermilion, who has the nature of blesses his devotees with abundant boons, he holds tusk, lasso and elephant goad, pomegranate, and with the same hand he shows gesture of protection. holds a modaka in his trunk. He is with omkarsarasvati and omkaralaksmi. He is standing on the seat of omkara.

The form in which lord ganesha created Omkara and Vedas and appeared before them pleased by their penance and granted them venerableness and the quality and right of being worshipped by all the worlds is called omkara Ganapati.

He is the lord of all knowledge and wisdom and is prayed for wisdom and knowledge.
