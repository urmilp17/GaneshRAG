45. Trimukha Ganapati

He is holding conch, trident, sacred vessel filled with water, a small drum, discus and rosary in his hands, bears the moon on his crest, has three eyes and is seated on Adisesha (the big white serpent with thousand heads. in the left, centre and right he has the faces of brahma, Vishnu and Shiva respectively. He has a body with a white lustre. The faces are golden, dark and clear in complexiom I meditate on the lord who is known as trimukha.

The creator of the tattvas (principles) refered by the name nara is called nara. The ganesha with human appearance is the one who has the form of three gunas. He is the one having the combined form of brahma (the creator), Vishnu the protector and mahesha (the destroyer rudra is refered here)

The form in which lord ganesha appeared before goddess parvati and sage atri with the faces of brahma Vishnu and rudra happily (pleased by their devotion), is trimukha Ganapati.

The lord is the giver of all accomplishments of life and all wealth.
