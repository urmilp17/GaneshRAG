94. Aghora Ganapati

He has a cruel face, fierce form, he dark blue in complexion, he rides a fierce bird sharabha. He holds vetala, vessel filled with drink, axe, elephant goad, lasso, sword, shield and trident. He has a curvy elephant face, bears moon on his crest and has three eyes, wears red clothes. He is called chandoddanda. 

I contemplate on him in my heart centre. Chandoddanda is Aghora Ganesha the manifestation of the celestial weapon aghorastra.
