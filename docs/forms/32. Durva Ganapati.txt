32. Durva Ganapati

He has a garland, his one tusk, lotus and axe in his hands, he has four hands and three eyes. He who is pleased by the devotion of durva grass (the deity of durva grass) he has 8 crown made of gems. He has reddish complexion and has red fragrant pastes (like red sandal paste) applied on his body, he is accompanied by moksalaksmi (the goddess who is the wealth of final liberation) and jnanavidya (the embodiment of the knowledge of ultimate wisdom) I contemplate on durvaganesa he has the moon as his crestal ornament and he who has a big belly.


goodesss doorva is is anna Shakti (the energy in food materials , physical energy) she is the daughter of brahma the creator. Once by the curse of durga, she descended to earth and turned into grass.

Pleased by the penance and austerity of doorva, lord ganesha blessed her and gave back to her her previous form (celestial divine form of the goddess). Lord ganesha also blessed her to stay in the form of grass (assuming another form) while also possessing the goddess form. He also granted her to be the most sacred among the leaves. He blessed her to be used in the worship of all the gods.(in her grass form). The lord also blessed her that she will be his favorite material of worship.

The form in which the lord ganesha appeared before doorva and blessed her with these wonderful boons is revered as Durva Ganapati. 

Once lord Ganesha assumed the form of an old man (vruddha Ganapati) and went to test and purify king
Janaka. After testing Janaka, the lord was pleased by the offering of durva grass of the poor Brahmin trishiraa, with which he sated his hunger. The lord blessed thrishiraa with wealth, ultimate wisdom of the self, final liberation in the end of his life.

The lord is the giver of food and food energy, giver of wisdom and liberation.
