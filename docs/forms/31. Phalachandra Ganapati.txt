31. Phalachandra Ganapati

His form is of the complexion of sindoora (vermilion). He holds kumuda flower, life giving herb, pomegranate and tusk in four of his hands, a pot of amruta (a death stopping elixir) in his
trunk.

He is elephant faced, he bears the moon in his crest, has three eyes. He is adorned by a red cloth and is accompanied by amrutasanjivini (the goddess of life giving herb) and amrutésvari (the goddess of amruta)

When Chandra the moon god was affected by the curse of ganesha himself, (because he once insulted ganesha, blinded by pride and arrogance, that he was the most handsome of the gods), he realised his fault and pleased ganesha through his penance. Ganesha appeared before him and blessed him with various boons and wore him (in the form of crescent moon) as an ornament on his forehead. Because of that
act, he became phalachandra - he who has the moon on his forehead.

Ganesha when worshiped in this form prevents untimely death and gives life (life energy)
