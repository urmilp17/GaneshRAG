79. Dwara Ganapati

I worship that Dwara Ganapati who has the hue of japapuspa (hibiscus flower), has lasso, the life gem, tusk and mokada sweet, and holds a pot filled with sudha - the celestial nectar which stops death, who has elephant face, three eyes. and is accompanied by goddess dwaralaksmi.

There are four entrances, dharma dvara (entrance of righteousness) , artha dvara (entrance of wealth) kama dvara (entrance of desire) moksa dvara ( entrance of liberation). These are the entrances to attain the four accomplishments.

The deities of these four entrances are Vishnu, shambhu, Shakti and surya respectively. The sub deities of the entrances are yajna, skanda, mara (manmatha) and krodamukha (varaha). The supreme mother who is the mother of these four entrance deities is called dvaralaksmi. The lord and husband of dvaralaksmi is dwara Ganapati.

He is the giver of the four great accomplishments of life (dharma - righteousness, artha - wealth, kama- desire and moksha - liberation)
