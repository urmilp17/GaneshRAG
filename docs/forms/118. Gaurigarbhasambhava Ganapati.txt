118. Gaurigarbhasambhava Ganapati

He has the skin tone of noon plant's flower, and that of a cloud in the sandhya time, he holds lasso, elephant goad, lotus and axe in his hands, he is elephant faced, has rat symbol on his flag, he was born from the womb of gauri.  He has three eyes, and two tusks, I contemplate on him who has a vermilion mark on his forehead, the lord who killed the demon named sindoora.

The lord ganesha who incarnated from the womb of goddess Shakti and destroyed the demon sundoora is gaurigarbhasambhava.

He is the form of ganesha who rectifies problems related to pregnancy and problems during pregnancy.
