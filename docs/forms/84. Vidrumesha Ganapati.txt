84. Vidrumesha Ganapati

I worship the  pravalavighnanaya (vidrume ganesha) who has an elephant face, holds ten weapons in his hands and wears the moon on his crest, he wears a red garment that’s red in color like the hibiscus flower, he is adorned with shiny ornaments made of precious gems, one of the tusks of the lord is broken, he is accompanied with sarvahrdyabodhini and vinastavastudayini.

By the penance of the goddess Adi Shakti (the embodiment of supreme power), he emerged from the coral (pravala) as the result of adisakti’s meditation. He is called pravalagananayaka.

The lord id the one who blesses us back with all the things we have lost and also the wisdom of everyones heart and mind.
