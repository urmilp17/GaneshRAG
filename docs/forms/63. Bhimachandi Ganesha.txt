63. Bhimachandi Ganesha

The lord is attractive as countless manmathas. He has a big belly, he wears yellow cloth and shawl. He bears veena a string instrument, rosary, trident, parrot, bow, book and arrow in eight hands, bears a moon in his crown, he rides a parrot, has three eyes, he has the skin complexion of emerald I contemplate on him the bhimacandiganésa who has a great elephant trunk.


He who was meditated upon and worshiped by goddess chandi for killing shumbha and others and granted powers to chandi is bhimacandiganesa. He blesses his devotees with victory.
