30. Nidhiprada Ganapati

He is in golden complexion, has an elephant face, he shows the gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection and holds lasxo and elephant goad in his four hands. He lives in the golden house, he is handsome, has the moon on his crest and is wearing an yellow garment, he is always with the goddesses rddhi and samrddhi, he is served by shanka and other deities (shanka, Padma and other deities of ce-
lestial treasures) he is known by the name svarnakarsana, he is surrounded by vasumati and other goddesses (the wives of the shanka and other treasure gods). I contemplate on him.

Once all the wealth of the gods were eaten by fire (burnt in fire). The lord of the yakshas (Kubera - the keeper of wealth) and other gods worshiped lord ganesha through austerity and penance. Pleased by their austerity ganesha appeared before them and gave nine kinds of celestial treasures to kubera.

Nidhiprada Ganapati is the lord of all treasures and the giver of all treasures to his worshipers.
