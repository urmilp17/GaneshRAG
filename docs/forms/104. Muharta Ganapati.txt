104. Muharta Ganapati

He is of golden complexion and with his hands he shows the gesture of wish giving and gesture of protection and holds two lotuses. He wears a shiny yellow silk garment, he is adorned with great ornaments that are embellished with precious gems. He is accompanied by jyotirvidya (the goddesses of astrology) he is elephant faced, wears the moon on his crest and has three eyes, he resides in the centre of jyotiscakra. I contemplate on, think of that Muhurta Ganapati.

Hiranya purusha the parameshwara (supreme lord) beyond the three stages
(three gunas) appeared from the mediation done by brahma the creator the jyotiscakra on brahma's request.

To create the planets, hiranya purusha started meditation on ganesha and worshipped him with deep austerity and penance, which is generally hard to accomplish.

The lord ganesha who emerged from the meditation of hiranya purusha is muhurta gananayaka.

This form of ganesha is worshiped to pacify the ill effects of adverse planetary positions in one’s life.
