2. Shakti Ganapati

I worship the shaktivighnesvara (Shakti Ganesha) who holds lasso, elephant goad, pomegranate fruit and tusk in his four hands, and holds the modaka sweet in his trunk and wears a golden garment, has golden complexion. The lord bears the moon on his crest, having an elephant face and having three eyes, he is embraced by herambaa (all mother) the parasakti (the embodiment of supreme power of the supreme absolute god) who is sitting on his left lap.
