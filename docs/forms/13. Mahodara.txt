13. Mahodara

The lord is elephant faced, has three eyes, he bears the moon on his crest, he is as white as sphatika stone. He shows the gesture of wisdom (chinmudra), holds a rosary, a lasso and a sharply pointed elephant goad. He is seated in the posture of virasana. (heroic posture) He is accompanied with bramhavidya and jnanasiddhi. I contemplate on mahodara Ganesha. The embodiment of ultimate wisdom.

Mahodara Ganapati is the brahman in the form of bodha (ultimate wisdom and true experience of the self)

Mahodara is prayed for freedom from the great delusion of worldliness and duality.
