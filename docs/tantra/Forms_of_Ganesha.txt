About The Book

Vinayaka Tantra is one of the tantras
(the text which deals with the worship
of different forms of lord Ganeha.) lord
Ganesha is worshiped as the supreme
absolute god, the supreme reality from
whom all other gods and goddesses
emerged as per the ganapatya lore.
There are more than hundred and
twenty forms of Ganesha described in
the Vinayaka tantra and each one of
these forms of lord Ganesha are wor-
shiped for a unique and specific pur-
pose.

Ganesha is the supreme reality and
hence he is the atman (soul) of every
god and goddess. There are forms
of Ganesha who was worshipped by
different gods and goddesses that are
described in this tantra.

In this book, all the verses of con-
templation and visualization (dhyana
shlokas) of different forms of Ganesha
which are given in the vinayaka tantra
are given along with transliteration in
IAST format and translation in English.
